Google quietly turned your phone's camera into a live AI you can talk to — point it at anything, and it answers you back, right away.

It's called Search Live. It's already built into the regular Google app (not a separate download), it's free, and no sign-up is needed. Tap the Live icon right under the search bar, turn on the camera, and start talking — it sees what you see and answers out loud, back and forth, like a real conversation.

It runs on Google's newest live AI model, using your camera and your voice at the same time. It just expanded to over 200 countries this year, on both iPhone and Android.

The honest limit: it still gets things wrong sometimes, and it's not a substitute for an actual expert — but for "what even is this thing," it's genuinely useful.
