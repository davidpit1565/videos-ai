## 2. Her method as a checklist
1. **Hook**: one job, stop the scroll.
2. **Promise**: right after the hook, say what the viewer gets if they stay (she does it in the first ~10 s: "I promise you, this is the best structure").
3. **Value 1**: the first useful thing, delivered early, not after a long setup.
4. **Open loop 1**: straight after value 1, one spoken line that says something bigger is still coming ("and that's still not the worst part"). It works as a second hook.
5. **Value 2**, then **open loop 2**: the same pair again ("you run it twice").
6. **Value 3**: the strongest one, last.
7. **CTA**: a real next step for the people who stayed to the end (comment keyword, DM), not a throwaway.
