Claude Ultra is free for 6 months, if you work on open-source software.

Anthropic, the company behind Claude, runs a program called Claude for Open Source. It gives open-source maintainers and contributors 6 months of Claude Ultra on the house. In the video we say the plan normally costs $200 a month; the program page itself shows no price, so check your own plan page. There are up to 10,000 spots.

You qualify if you meet ANY ONE of these:
1. You maintain a project with 500+ dependent repos, 100+ dependent packages or 200,000+ monthly downloads.
2. You are a core contributor to a recognized foundation or language project.
3. You have 100+ merged pull requests into repos you do not own, in the last 12 months.
4. You have a repo with 20+ external contributors who got pull requests merged in the last 12 months.
5. You have a repo with an OpenSSF criticality score of 0.4 or higher.

The general requirements, for everyone: a GitHub account at least 2 years old, public open-source activity in the last 90 days, a project under an OSI-approved license, and you must be 18 or older.

Do not fit a number? The page says to apply anyway and tell them why your project matters to the ecosystem. That is a discretionary track: nobody promises it.

How it works: sign in with GitHub, tell them about your project, wait for the email. Only approved people are contacted. Activate within 90 days. The 6 months start at activation, then your paid plan pauses and resumes. Overage charges are possible. Anthropic can close the program at any time.

This is NOT guaranteed. Apply with real numbers only; inflated metrics get you disqualified. We did not apply ourselves.

Sources: https://claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss and https://www.anthropic.com/claude-for-oss-terms (read 18 November 2026). Full steps are in the link in bio.
