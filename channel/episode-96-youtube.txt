Title: This AI solved two physics problems (what Anthropic says)

Description:
Anthropic says its new model, Claude Fable 5.2, solved two open problems about black holes: a calculation set out in 2003 that was too long to finish, and the exact limit of a method physicists have used for more than 15 years.
These are Anthropic's own claims from its launch post. Outside scientists have not checked them yet, and we have not tested this ourselves.
