One word can wreck a whole explanation.

Here's a real example: "Add this to the column." Said once, never explained — and
it's exactly the kind of line that makes total sense to whoever wrote it, and loses
everyone else.

There's a 5-second test that catches this every time: read the line out loud, then
ask "what did I just say — in your own words?" Never yes or no. If the answer
doesn't come back right, the line isn't done.

The real before and after:
Before: "Add this to the column."
After: "Instagram fills this box on its own."
