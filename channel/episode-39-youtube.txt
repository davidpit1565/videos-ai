Your smart TV isn't just playing what's on. It's watching what you watch — and sending that back.

It's called Automatic Content Recognition (ACR). Most smart TVs run it by default: the TV periodically fingerprints what's on screen and sends that fingerprint back to the manufacturer to build an ad and viewing profile. It picks up content from anything plugged into the TV — a game console, a cable box, a streaming stick — not just the TV's own built-in apps. Some brands check in as often as four times a minute, on anything you're watching.

One setting in the TV's own menu turns it off — it's different per brand, and the full setup covers Samsung, LG, Vizio, and Roku.

It won't stop the ads. It just stops them learning from your own screen.
