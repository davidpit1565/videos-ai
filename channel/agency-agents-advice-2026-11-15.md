**AI ADVICE, not a plan David approved and not measured.** Written by Claude acting in turn as five role prompts copied from github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents (commit 8ae6164, MIT), installed read-only in `.claude/agents/aw-*.md`. Nothing here was tested. Every number is either quoted from our own files (source named) or marked ASSUMPTION. No prices, clients or results are invented: there are none to cite (positioning Q7, 13.11: "nothing he built for a client yet").

Question: *Given our files, what are the 5 concrete next steps to earn real money from this channel within 60-90 days, in order, with what to verify first?*

VAT registration (small-business exemption up to EUR 25,000/yr) is the one bureaucratic step that blocks an invoice; age-18 self-employment eligibility was UNVERIFIED there.

- Funnel (`content-funnel.md`): the low-funnel offer is OPEN ("without a real offer, low-funnel videos have nothing honest to call to action"); top-funnel reach did not convert (ep86 +1 follower, ep82 +1, ep80 0).

**What all five advisors agreed on:** in 60-90 days the channel itself will not pay. Engine B needs about 5,000 subscribers (plan) and we have an unknown, small number; reel monetisation for a ~335-follower account is not researched (OPEN in positioning Q3). The only realistic money in that window is engine A: selling a small, fixed AI system to a business, with the channel used as *proof that David builds and tests real things*, not as the source of buyers.

## The five steps, in order

### 1. Verify the blockers before selling anything (week 1)
*Business Strategist: "start with the problem; execution feasibility is part of the strategy."*
Verify first, with an accountant or VLAIO/an ondernemingsloket, not from the web: (a) can David, 18 and employed elsewhere, invoice as a side business (bijberoep) in Flanders, and what does registration cost; (b) VAT registration and the small-business exemption; (c) what his employment contract says about side work (non-compete / exclusivity clause). Also decide with David whether money is a goal in these 90 days at all: on 13.11 he said reach comes first. **No outreach until (a)-(c) are answered.** Cost and duration: UNKNOWN until asked.

### 2. Define ONE fixed-scope offer with a published price (week 1-2)
*Offer & Lead Gen Strategist (value equation) + Pricing Analyst ("never price in a vacuum").*
One narrow system, one price, one end date, e.g. "an AI assistant that answers your incoming enquiries/e-mails in your tone, live in 2 weeks" (example only, not validated). Build the price from: our only market data (Codelines from EUR 2,500 for a discovery sprint; plan range EUR 1,500-8,000; freelance rates quoted in the plan), David's real hours (ASSUMPTION: unknown until he times one build), and a first-client discount stated honestly as "first project, in exchange for permission to publish it as a case study". The Pricing Analyst insists on a cost floor first: David must log the hours of the first build. Guarantee: something we can honour, e.g. "you pay after it works on your own data" (ASSUMPTION: a choice for David, not tested). Put the price on the site (the plan's own insight: almost nobody in Flanders publishes prices).
*Rejected from the prompts:* the "≥10x value-to-price", "LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1" and "30% conversion" targets are the prompts' own unsourced benchmarks; they say nothing about our market.

### 3. Warm outreach to people David already knows, as interviews, not pitches (week 2-4)
*Offer & Lead Gen ("warm outreach first to validate the offer") + Discovery Coach (SPIN / gap questions).*
List every small business owner David or his family/friends know personally (Flanders and the Hebrew-speaking circle the plan names as "the first client"). Ask for 20-minute conversations: "how does X work for you today, where does it break, what does that cost you per week?" Use the Discovery Coach's call shape (agenda first, 60-70% on their current state, a "no" is a fine outcome). Goal: find ONE repeated, costly, boring task. The plan's original target was 10 conversations in week 1 and 20 more in weeks 2-3; how many leads David actually has is UNKNOWN: verify by writing the list before promising a number. Conversion from conversation to project: ASSUMPTION (the plan assumed 5%, unmeasured).

### 4. Turn the first real conversation into a one-page proposal, then build and publish it (week 4-10)
*Proposal Strategist (mirror their words, cost of inaction, one proof point) + Business Strategist (decision gates).*
One page: their problem in their own words, what it costs them (their number, from the call), what will be built, what is out of scope, price, timeline, what happens if it breaks. The only honest proof today is our own production system (the plan already proposes it as "case study 1, labelled internal project") and the episodes that show real tests; never a client result we do not have. After delivery: the project becomes a site case study and an episode *only with the client's written permission*, including what broke (the plan's rule). Decision gate (ASSUMPTION, for David to set): if after ~30 conversations nobody wants the offer, change the offer, not the amount of outreach.

### 5. Give the channel one honest low-funnel next step, then measure it (from week 2, in parallel)
*Offer & Lead Gen ("never build capture you can't honour") + Business Strategist ("what to stop").*
Close the OPEN question in `content-funnel.md`: the low end becomes (a) the free setup guide / newsletter on the site and (b) a plain "I build these for businesses: see the price" page linked from the profile. One mid/low-funnel reel in five (the funnel's own ratio) points at it, e.g. a saveable system the viewer can copy (89-style save value) plus "I'll build it for your business". Measure: profile-link clicks, newsletter sign-ups, and enquiries per reel (all three are not yet tracked as such: verify what `/api/track` and Beehiiv can show before promising a dashboard). Stop counting follower numbers as progress while the bought ~250 are in them.

## Disagreements and risks the advisors raised

- **Goal conflict:** David's stated goal (reach first) vs this question (income first). Strategist's view: both can run, but the hours go to step 3, not to more reels, if money matters in 90 days. David decides.
- **Audience mismatch:** the reels attract curious consumers aged 18-34 (ep72 Insights in `content-memory.md`: 18-24 47.3%, 25-34 26.7%; one episode), not business owners; the plan already said English reels build an audience and Flemish/Dutch contact sells. Do not expect reel viewers to buy EUR 1,500+ systems.
- **Time:** David is employed elsewhere; one client project plus 4-5 reels a week may not fit. ASSUMPTION, unmeasured.
- **Legal/admin:** steps 1(a)-(c) are unverified and can block everything.
- **Prompt quality:** the agency-agents prompts are generic consulting/sales playbooks with US/enterprise examples and unsourced benchmark numbers. Their value here was structure (value equation, discovery questions, one-page proposal), not facts.

## Not used / why
- Reel or creator-fund income, sponsorships, affiliate links: not researched for an account this size (positioning Q3 OPEN); sponsorship needs the ~5,000-subscriber newsletter the plan names. Research first, then decide.
- Courses / paid products: the plan says "after ten free systems"; not in a 90-day window.
