# Formats, not individual clips (Roni via David, 9.11.2026)
A FORMAT = a named, numbered series with a locked template (intro frame, title style, colour, sound, structure, length) and ONE variable slot (the topic). A viewer should recognise episode N+1 in the first second. Every video from now on is an episode of a format; a one-off clip needs a reason.
Spec each format with: name and promise (1 line), funnel stage (top/mid/low), locked elements (visual, title animation, sound sting, beat structure), the variable slot, numbering, length, cost to produce, what a viewer does next, risk, what evidence is missing.
Evidence for formats working: numbered series in the references (arising.ai 'Banned Prompt #93', crcle.ai 'Prohibited Prompt #N' with 1.1M-1.8M views on pinned posts: both from David's screenshots/reels, views unverified by us). Our own top 3 are one-off scenes, not a series, and the follow-ups did not repeat them (ep 73, 78, 79, 81: 221-332 views), so a format must be tested over several episodes before judging.
