## Claims verified live (9.10.2026), first-party

YouTube Help, support.google.com/youtube/answer/16422785 (no date on the page, © 2026 Google):
- An AI "age estimation model" decides whether a user is over or under 18: "We do this regardless of the birthdate in their account in order to put protections in place for more teens on YouTube."
- Countries: "Australia, Brazil, Canada, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Switzerland, Turkey, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom, the US, and countries in the European Economic Area."
- Protections for an account identified as under 18: age-restricted content blocked; digital wellbeing (break and bedtime reminders on by default); safeguards for recommendations and search results; safety reminders; non-personalized ads only; autoplay prompt; podcasts limit.
- If wrong: "you can always verify your age by submitting your government ID, credit card or selfie through a verification process in your Google Account."
- The page describes NO notification telling a user their age was estimated.
YouTube Help, support.google.com/youtube/answer/3159776: how to check your Google Account birthday: myaccount.google.com/privacy > Personal info > Birthday. This page does NOT mention the AI estimate and does not say it applies to flagged users.
