## 5. /e/N setup steps (English only)
1. Open the Facebook app on your phone (iPhone or Android; not the website). 
2. Tap your profile picture (bottom right on iPhone, top right on Android).
3. Tap Settings & Privacy, then Settings.
4. Tap Preferences, then Camera roll sharing suggestions. If it is missing, your account may not have the feature: you are done.
5. Look at "Get camera roll suggestions when you're browsing Facebook" and any "cloud processing" toggle. Blue/on means active.
6. Switch them off. Expect the toggles to turn grey.
7. Optional, strongest: phone Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Facebook > None (iPhone), or Settings > Apps > Facebook > Permissions > Photos and videos > Don't allow (Android). Expect Facebook to ask for photo access if you later post a photo.
Menu names move between app versions; page says "verified Nov 2026".
