## Product / claims verified live (9.10.2026)

- **Microsoft Privacy Statement, "Last Updated: September 2026"** (microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement),
  section "Artificial Intelligence and Copilot capabilities":
  - "Microsoft Copilot also uses prompts and related data to provide and improve services, including relevant advertising."
  - "we may use your data to develop, train, and fine-tune our AI models, including large language models (LLMs)."
  This is the account-wide statement, so it holds whichever Copilot app version the viewer has.
- **New Copilot app (available from 18.8.2026), Microsoft support "privacy controls" page**
  (support.microsoft.com/en-US/Privacy/microsoft-copilot/privacy-controls): Settings > Personalization >
  "Allow ads personalization" toggle; "Saved memories" toggle in the same place. Copilot does not show
  personalized ads to users under 18.
- **Account-wide ads switch:** account.microsoft.com/privacy/ad-settings > "See ads that interest you".
- **Incogni ranking (episode 63's source):** Copilot among the highest-risk AI chats.

## UNCONFIRMED — deliberately NOT said in the video

- "Training on by default", the "Training on conversation activity" toggle, "kept 18 months" and "some chats
  reviewed by people, no opt-out" all come from Microsoft pages that, since 18.8.2026, say they apply ONLY to
  the older Copilot app. The new-app pages don't mention training at all. Not used until confirmed on the
  new app itself.
- Work/school (Entra) accounts and Microsoft 365 apps for home have different rules (Microsoft says those
  prompts are not used to train foundation models) — so the share line targets a personal laptop, not work.
