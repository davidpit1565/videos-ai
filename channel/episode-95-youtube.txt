Title: ChatGPT draws you as a cartoon (the prompt)

Description:
How: open ChatGPT, type "Create a caricature of me and my job based on everything you know about me." and watch it draw you.
Tip: tell it a bit about yourself first and skip uploading your real photo.
The drawing in this video is a drawn example, not a real ChatGPT result.
