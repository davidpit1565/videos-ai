Title: Your ChatGPT caricature comes with a privacy warning

Description:
The trend: ask ChatGPT "Create a caricature of me and my job based on everything you know about me." (wording as published by Forbes, 9 Feb 2026).
What Forbes reports: a security CEO says users keep adding personal details until the result looks right; experts advise skipping real photos and keeping the prompt generic; memory (saved memories and chat history) is off by default for users in the UK and EU as of Forbes' February reporting; safer: describe yourself in words, or use Temporary Chat.
The drawing in this video is a drawn example, not a real ChatGPT result.
