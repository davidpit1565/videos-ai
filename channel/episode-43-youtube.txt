Windows can now take a screenshot of your entire screen every few seconds — and save it.

Microsoft built a real feature called Recall. It's an AI model that turns every one of those screenshots into a searchable history of everything you've ever done on your PC — every app, every chat, every website.

It's off by default on Copilot+ PCs, and Microsoft locks it behind Windows Hello — your face or fingerprint, re-checked every time you open it.

But this April, a security researcher published a free tool, TotalRecall Reloaded, that walks right past that lock and pulls every screenshot Recall has ever taken off the machine.

One setting in Windows tells you if it's already turned on, and how to check what it's actually saved.
