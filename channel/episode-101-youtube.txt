6 free alternatives to apps you pay for (Loom, Notion, Calendly) #shorts

Six apps people pay for every month, and a free, open-source alternative for each one:
1. Loom to Cap (screen videos)
2. Notion to AppFlowy (notes, docs, AI)
3. Calendly to Cal.diy (booking page, personal use only)
4. Intercom to Chatwoot (customer chat, free if you host it yourself)
5. Wispr Flow to OpenWhispr (you talk, it types)
6. ElevenLabs to VoiceStudio (AI voices on your own computer, only 7 months old)

We did not test these. They are alternatives, not full replacements, and some need your own server. Check each one first.
Added up, the cheapest paid plan of each of these six apps is about $75 a month per person (official pricing pages, November 2026).
