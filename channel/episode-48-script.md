# Episode 48 — script, ready to produce

## Topic
Apple's Communication Safety (part of Screen Time / Family Sharing on a Child Account)
uses on-device machine learning to detect nudity, gore, and violence in photos and videos
sent or received in Messages and FaceTime video calls on a child's Apple device, and blurs
them behind a warning. Apple's own support page states the analysis happens entirely on
the device: Apple does not receive the photo or video, and no signal — not even an
indication that something was flagged — leaves the device. For a child under 13, viewing
the blurred content requires a parent or guardian to enter the family's Screen Time
passcode. It is on by default for a Child Account under 18 (age defaults vary by region).

**Verified 23.9.2026, live:** Apple's own newsroom post, "Apple's new child safety features
now available" (apple.com/newsroom, dated 14.9.2026 — ten days old), confirms the expansion
to gore/violence detection (previously nudity only) and coverage of live FaceTime video
calls, shipping in iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and macOS 27. Cross-checked against Apple Support
article 105069 ("About Communication Safety on your child's Apple device") for the
mechanism, the on-device/no-signal-leaves-the-device claim, and the under-13 passcode gate.

### Scope (hard limits for this script — do not widen)
- Only Apple's Communication Safety, on a Child Account inside Family Sharing, on Apple
  devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro).
- Only covers Messages and FaceTime video calls — do not imply it scans WhatsApp, Snapchat,
  Instagram DMs, email, or any other app or website.
- Do not claim Apple, or anyone, is notified when content is flagged — the sourced claim is
  the opposite: no signal leaves the device, to anyone, including Apple.
- The under-13 Screen Time passcode gate is what Apple's own page states for children under
  13 specifically — do not claim the exact same mechanism applies identically to every teen,
  since Apple's own documentation says defaults vary by age/region and does not fully detail
  the 13-17 case in the same terms.
- Do not claim this catches everything a parent would worry about (it is nudity/gore/
  violence in two specific apps' media — not cyberbullying, not predatory text, not location).
- Requires the child to actually be set up as a Child Account under Family Sharing — a
  teenager on their own adult-converted Apple ID is a different, unstated case.

## Production notes
- **Accent-drift gate (`check_accent.py`):** line 3 first came out scoring not-american
  0.99 (file median 0.016) — a real, ear-relevant outlier, not noise. Fixed by re-seeding
  that line alone (`--line-seeds "3:2077"`) until the classifier read it as clean
  (0.00-0.02 across every subsequent run). No wording change needed for this one.
- **Swallowed-word gate (`voice_doctor.py --deep`):** the original lines 2 and 4 ("...
  expanded just over a week ago..." / "...not any other app.") both reproducibly flagged
  a real word as rushed/clipped ("expanded," "other") across every seed tried. Fixed by
  minimal, fact-preserving rewording rather than continued rerolling — "expanded" →
  "updated ... too", "not any other app" → "nothing else." Both cleared cleanly and
  stayed clean across every subsequent regeneration.
- **Line 5's opening word — the hard case:** whichever word opened this exact line
  ("Under," then "Kids," then "Anyone") kept measuring as rushed/clipped regardless of
  the word's identity, length, seed, exaggeration, or the rate-correction floor — eight
  independent mechanical attempts (reseeds, a rate-floor override, an exaggeration bump,
  two rewordings) all failed on the same structural pattern, meaning the defect was the
  line's own rendering slot, not any one word. Resolved with `audio/line_doctor.py`
  exactly as it's built for: generated 12 candidate takes of the line, picked the one
  with the slowest measured delivery of the opening word (ranked by speaking rate, not
  the tool's default tail-energy ranking, since this defect is about duration/rate, not
  a swallowed consonant tail), and locked it into
  `audio/voice/profile/canonical-lines.json` (`canon-reel48-line5.wav`) so `build_voice.py`
  loads it byte-for-byte on every future render instead of regenerating. Confirmed clean
  on `voice_doctor.py --deep` afterward: "rushed / clipped: none."
- The wording changes above are reflected in the Narration list, `video/reel-48.html`'s
  CUES array, and this file — all three stayed in sync.
