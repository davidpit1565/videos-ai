Claude just destroyed paid apps with 11 free skills.

The Replica skill is a free, open-source pack of 11 Claude skills that works like a small dev team: it studies an app, rebuilds it screen by screen, sets up the backend and tests it for bugs.

The 11, in order: recon, architect, design, build, backend, test, diff, entrepreneur, brand, launch, deploy.

The wildest one is the Entrepreneur skill: it reads real user reviews to find the missing features and the things people hate, then fixes them in your version.

It also rebrands your version, so it is your own brand and not a copy. It rebuilds what an app does, never its code, logo or copy.

Install: paste the repo link into Claude and say "install skill". The exact steps, the links and the limits are in the link in bio.

Limits, said plainly: it is a free pack of instructions, not a finished app. You still pay for hosting, a database, payments and a domain, and a Claude plan. Big apps take weeks. We did not run it ourselves for this video; everything above comes from the project's own page. Check the trademark and terms rules before you sell anything.

Save this for the next subscription you want to cancel, and send it to someone who pays for too many apps.
