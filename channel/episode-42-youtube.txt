Your phone might already have an AI that can catch a scammer mid-call — it's just turned off.

Google built a real feature called Scam Detection. It runs an AI model right on your phone during a live call, listening for the exact patterns scammers use — urgency, requests for gift cards or passwords, someone claiming to be your bank or the government. If it's suspicious, your phone alerts you with a sound and a notification, right in the middle of the call.

It's fully on-device — nothing about the call, no audio, no transcript, is ever sent to Google.

One setting turns it on. It's off by default, and right now it only works on certain Pixel phones — check your own settings to see if you have it.
