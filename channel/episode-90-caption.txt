My idea has 3 flaws. I show AI.

One AI cheers like a golden retriever: "Perfect idea!" The other stares like a tabby cat and lists the problems: "3 problems. Fix #1 first."

This is a joke about tone, not a test result. We tested that ChatGPT tends to agree with you (episodes 72, 73 and 80). We never tested Claude on this, so no, this does not prove one is better. The Claude cat is just how the meme plays it.

Real tip, no joke: whichever AI you use, ask it "What are the 3 biggest flaws in this idea?" before you ask "Is it good?"
