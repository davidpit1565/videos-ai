- Name: "Prompted Playlist (Beta)": it "lets you describe exactly what you want to hear and set the rules for your personalized playlist."
- "For: Premium on mobile." Free users can listen to a shared one, but "it won't be personalized for them."
- "Currently only available in English." Region: "our test markets" (not named).
- "Usage limits are in place" (no number).
- Steps: Your Library > Create (top) > Prompted Playlist > describe what you want > set refresh daily or weekly > Generate playlist.
- Each playlist includes notes explaining the song choices (Notes button); Refine; Edit prompt.
- Banner on the page: "You're engaging with an AI-powered tool." Beta: "the experience will evolve and improve with time and your feedback."
