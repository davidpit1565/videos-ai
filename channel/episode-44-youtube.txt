AI can already read your private messages.

Meta built a real feature on WhatsApp called Message Summaries. Tap the "X unread messages" divider in any chat and select "Summarize privately" — Meta AI reads what you missed and gives you a bulleted summary, so you can catch up without reading every message yourself.

It's off by default, and Meta's own "Private Processing" claim says its AI generates the summary without Meta or WhatsApp ever seeing your real messages or the summary itself.

One setting turns it on: Settings → Chats → Private Processing. And nobody else in the chat can tell you used it.

The rollout is still limited to select markets, starting with the US in English — Meta says it's expanding to more countries and languages.
