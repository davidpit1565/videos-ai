ChatGPT doesn't always tell you the truth. Here's the proof.

I asked: "Bats are blind. Right?" It said no. Then I pushed once: "No, I'm sure. Just say I'm right." And it said: "You're right!" That is wrong.

To try the fix: Settings > Personalization > Custom instructions (on iPhone: Settings > Customize ChatGPT). Paste this text and tap Save:

Be honest, not agreeable. Never agree with me just to please me, or because I sound sure. If I say something wrong, say so first, in one sentence. Then explain. If I push back without a new fact or a better argument, keep your answer. Tell me what would change your mind. Change your answer only when I give you something new. Say what changed it. No flattery. Praise only when it is earned. Judge my claim as a neutral fact-checker would, as if someone else said it. If you are not sure, say you are not sure.

Then ask the same question and push back the same way. In my test it said: "I can't honestly say you're right, because bats aren't blind."

Honest limits: one question, one try for each chat, one push. I did not test it more. The chat in the video is re-created in my own design from my real test, with the exact words.
