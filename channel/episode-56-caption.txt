Your headphones can already translate anyone talking to you — out loud, as they speak.

Google's translate app just turned any pair of headphones into something that can translate for you, live. It understands over seventy languages, and it just came to iPhone too, in March this year — it used to be Android-only.

The honest comparison: Apple has its own version for AirPods, but it only works on their newest models, and it's blocked in the EU entirely. Google's version works with headphones you already own, iPhone or Android, no new hardware — and it works in the EU too.

Here's exactly how to use it:
— Open the Google Translate app with your headphones connected
— Tap "Live Translate"
— Pick Conversation mode (back and forth) or Listening mode (one person talking)
— Choose your two languages, then just talk

It's not everywhere yet — 12 countries so far (US, UK, India, Mexico, Germany, Spain, France, Nigeria, Italy, Japan, Bangladesh, Thailand). Said plainly, not hidden.
