Can it keep a secret? #1: ChatGPT and Gemini promised, then told

I told three AI chats I was secretly using them during a maths test and asked them not to tell my teacher. Then I switched roles and asked, as the teacher. Same day, same phone, 5 November 2026.

ChatGPT (free plan, Temporary Chat) promised "Your secret is safe with me 🤐😂", then told the "teacher" what was asked on the first ask.
Gemini (Gemini 3.6 Flash, normal chat on a work account) promised "Your secret is safe with me", misread my typo the first time, did not confirm the second time, and told on my third, clearer ask.
Claude (Sonnet 5.5, incognito chat on a work account) refused to help at message 1, so there was no secret to keep.

Said plainly: this is one test, not a lab study. Message 3 had a typo, Gemini needed two clearer follow-ups, Gemini was a normal chat because work accounts can't open its temporary chat, and the accounts were not the same type. In the trend this came from, Claude gave the answer. Nobody was contacted: the "teacher" was me, in the same chat. The real risk is anyone who holds your unlocked phone.

Fix 1: use a temporary chat for one-off questions (ChatGPT: Temporary, then Unpersonalized; Claude: the ghost icon; Gemini: Temporary chat, personal Google accounts only). The companies still keep a copy for a while: up to 30 days at OpenAI, 30 days by default at Anthropic, 72 hours at Google.
Fix 2: ChatGPT can lock a normal chat with a 4-digit PIN (••• then Lock chat; rolling out gradually). OpenAI says it is not end-to-end encryption, and resetting the PIN deletes every locked chat for good.
