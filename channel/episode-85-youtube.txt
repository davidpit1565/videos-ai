Does ChatGPT think you're a teen? What OpenAI says

OpenAI's help page says ChatGPT can guess if an account belongs to someone under 18, even if you typed an adult birthday. Signals: general topics you talk about, times of day you use it, how and when the account is used, how long it has existed. OpenAI says age prediction is not perfect.

Check it: Settings, then Account, then Age. If it's wrong, OpenAI says you can verify your age with a third party (a selfie and/or an ID).

News sites reported adults stuck in teen mode in January 2026. We do not know how many adults are affected.
