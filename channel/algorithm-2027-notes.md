### Instagram's own statements we found (for the rubric)

- Mosseri 22.1.2025 (see A4): top three signals watch time, likes, sends; "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." CONFIRMED BY INSTAGRAM (as reported by SMT with direct quotes).
- Mosseri 25.2.2025: Instagram looks at both percentage watched and raw seconds watched, so longer videos are not penalized (SMT https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-longer-video-watch-time-versus-completion-rate/740916/ ). Weights not given.
- Mosseri said sends per reach correlates most with overall reach "in my experience" (reported by SMT; I did not open the original). Treat as his statement, not a published weight.
- Hashtags: Mosseri has repeatedly said hashtags do not significantly increase reach (SMT coverage, quote: "They don't significantly increase your reach on Instagram, contrary to popular belief."). Not in the reel.
- Originality: Instagram removes aggregators from recommendations and favors original posts (TechCrunch 30.4.2024 https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators/ ); the 2023 explainer says reels already posted on Instagram and "low-resolution or watermarked reels" are made less visible. Mosseri admits it is classifier-based, "not knowing". Not in the reel.
- NOT FOUND: any Instagram statement about the first 3 seconds, about "foreshadowing", "context" or "simplicity". The help.instagram.com page and one creators.instagram.com URL did not load (empty / 404), so no Help Center quote.
- Unverified: Meta AI's 79% median skip and third-party benchmarks in content-memory.md; I did not verify third-party blogs (many cite each other; they disagree on whether sends or watch time lead).

### Honest read of the reel
His three "yes" items are all one idea: the viewer understands the video and wants to see the end. That is consistent with watch time being Instagram's top signal, but the reel gives no data, and the specific labels (foreshadowing, simplicity) are his framing, not Instagram's. His "no" items are plausible but unproven. The reel itself is a good format example (quiz with lock-in countdown, a visible score board), not evidence about the algorithm.

## 3. What this changes for @actually_works.ai

Rubric before publishing, in order of importance (M = measured by us afterwards, J = judgement before):

1. Subject understood from frame 0 and first spoken second by a blind reader (J; already a rule). Maps to his Context and to watch time.
2. A promise in the hook that the video keeps, and the answer arrives within about 10 s (J; his "make a promise, keep a promise").
3. One theme only, no second topic, no side explanation (J; his Simplicity).
4. Retention: skip rate under 60% and average watch above 8 s on Instagram insights; read at 48 h (M; our best have 40% / 13 s, median 79% / 4.3 s, Meta AI data, not independently checkable).
5. Sends per reach: a named person plus a reason to forward is on screen and spoken (J before, M after: sends per reach; our median is 0%, 45 of 50 reels have 0 shares).
6. A reason to watch to the end or twice (loop, quiz reveal at the very end) (J; quiz format delays the payoff on purpose).
7. Likes per reach (M; weaker signal for non-followers per Mosseri).
8. Saves: one screenshot-able card (M: saves; ep89 got 18).
9. Original, not a repost, no watermark, full resolution (J; Instagram does demote).
10. Caption and hashtag basics (few or none; they do not lift reach per Mosseri) (J).

Do NOT need attention: trending sounds as a reach lever (his claim and no Instagram support: keep music for feel per `music-notes.md`), daily posting for its own sake (no Instagram statement; our cadence of 4-5 a week stays, the real cost is quality), unexpected-twist quotas, hashtag research.

Conflicts in what we do today:
- Zero sends: we get 0 shares on 45 of 50 reels. Our endings rarely give a specific person to send to; ep97 does ("Send it to a buddy").
- Payoff-first and comprehension: ep88 ("never posted" last) and ep93 (quiet frame 0) lost most viewers in the first 2 s. Same lesson as his Context.
- Several of our reels are explainers or multi-part (second claim, side card); his Simplicity and Mosseri's watch-time logic both punish that.
- The ep97 caption uses six hashtags (`#ai #claude #anthropic #aiart #pelican #actuallyworks`) and a long block; per Mosseri hashtags do not matter for reach, so the caption can be shorter without cost. Its "Type A, B, C or D" comment prompt is consistent with the reel's lock-in quiz.
- His strongest transferable structure (an on-screen visible score and a lock-in countdown) is already planned in ep97's quiz (countdown, shuffled letters). Not proven for us; log as an experiment.
- Caveat on his reel: no numbers shown, one creator, so none of this is a measured method; our own 48 h and 72 h readings decide.
