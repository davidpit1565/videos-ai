Your kid's phone can now blur nudity and violence for them. It will never tell you when it does.

It's called Communication Safety, and Apple just expanded it (14.9.2026) to catch gore and violence too, not just nudity, in Messages and FaceTime. It runs entirely on the device — Apple's own page says it never sees the photo, and no signal leaves the phone. Not to you. Not to Apple.

Go check it right now, on your own phone:
1. Settings → your name at the top → Family
2. Tap your kid's name → Screen Time
3. Find Communication Safety — confirm it's ON (it's on by default for a Child Account, but confirm it, don't assume it)
4. Set a Screen Time Passcode only you know, if you haven't already — under 13, they need it from you before they can look past the blur
5. Know the real limit: it only covers Messages and FaceTime. Not WhatsApp. Not Snapchat. Not any other app.

That's it — that's the entire setting, right there, nothing else to click.
