OpenAI's own learning site tells you to start a ChatGPT chat with this sentence:

Before you answer, ask me 3 clarifying questions you need.

Where it is: OpenAI Academy, Quick Tips, Step 1 "Force clarification": https://academy.openai.com/en/public/clubs/small-business-ipf4m/resources/bellevue-small-business-jam-2026-03-02 (checked 10 November 2026).

How to use it: open ChatGPT, start a new chat, paste the sentence as your first message, press send, then answer the questions ChatGPT asks and write what you want.

Two more from the same page:
Step 2: I'm trying to get help with ____. Here's my prompt so far: ____. What details should I add to get a more useful result?
Step 3: Respond with: (1) ___ (2) ___ (3) ___ in a checklist / table.
