Every private thing you've ever typed into ChatGPT trains its next model — and that's the default. Not just chat history: the files you upload and the replies you get back too.

Switch it off, and every new conversation stays out of training for good. One catch: it only protects what happens after — nothing already sent gets pulled back out.

There's also a separate mode ("Temporary Chat") that skips training and history completely, every single chat, with nothing to remember.

Takes about ten seconds, one time.
