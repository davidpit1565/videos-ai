Every private thing you've ever typed into ChatGPT trains its next model — and that's the default. Not just chat history: the files you upload and the answers you get back too.

Turn it off, and everything you type after that stays out of training completely. But it only protects what happens after — nothing you already sent ever gets pulled back out.

There's also a separate mode that skips training and chat history completely, every single chat, with nothing to remember.

Takes about ten seconds, one time.
