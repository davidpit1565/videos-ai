# Ad plan: Super Ivy (UGC-ad marketplace), written 19.11.2026

Publish mode: AD (not an episode, no numbering, no channel promotion). Goal: downloads of the app. Nothing built, nothing published.
Sources (verified 19.11.2026): App Store listing id6759669515 (S1), superivy.com (S2), David's brief facts (S3).
Note on scores: they are judgements on the 200 views = 50 / 10,000 = 100 scale, not measurements. An ad's reach is bought or assigned by the marketplace, so the "views" part is only a proxy for how well the video holds a stranger.

## 1. TOPIC verdict
- Big problem: your hands are busy but the to-do list keeps growing (emails to answer, plans to add, things to remember), and typing it all into a phone is the chore.
- Why a stranger cares today: everyone has an inbox and a calendar; "just say it out loud" is understood in one second. App copy itself: "Your hands are busy. Your mind is not." (S1).
- Proof on screen: the real App Store icon and real screenshots, plus the app's own preview video, showing you speak and it drafts the email / adds the event.
- Weakness, said once: voice assistants already exist, so the difference is only "free, remembers you, imports memory from other AIs". Those three carry the ad.
- Funnel: LOW (direct response). The CTA is the point.

## 4. Beats, build-up, loop, sendable moment, CTA
| Beat | Time | What is NEW here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 hook (l1) | 0-2.5 | The problem and the product category: talk to your inbox |
| 2 name + price (l2-3) | 2.5-6 | Name and "free" land by 6 s (promise paid early) |

| 4 calendar (l6-7) | 10-14 | Second job, different screen, same layout |
| 5 hands full (l8-9) | 14-17 | The felt situation: it works without touching the phone |
| 6 memory (l10) | 17-21 | New ability: it knows your people and projects (a loop line is true: "one more thing that sets it apart") |
| 7 import (l11) | 21-25 | Newest and most unusual: three real AI logos flow in |
| 8 CTA (l12-13) | 25-29 | Icon returns, price, App Store |
- Build-up check: question opened in l1 is answered by beat 3 with real UI; every beat adds a new capability, none restates; the memory-import beat is saved for last on purpose. Flat risk: beats 3 and 4 look alike, so make beat 4 a different gesture (the event card slides into a week row).
- Loop/rewatch: last frame equals frame 0 (icon + wave), and the final spoken words "just talk" echo the hook's "just talk".
- Saveable moment: one card at beat 7, "Bring your memories from: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok" (S2 list), screenshot-able.
- Sendable to: a named person ("send to the friend who dictates everything while cooking"): suggest in caption, not on screen.
- CTA: "Download Super Ivy on the App Store" (spoken and on end card). No "link in bio" unless the marketplace gives a link; ask it.

## 6. Voice
- Recommendation: a woman's voice. Reason: the product's assistant is "she" (S1) and the ad is about talking to a voice; a warm, quick female narrator matches the subject. This is a judgement, no data.
- What I do NOT know: which stock or cloned voices exist in the repo beyond David's profile (`audio/voice/profile/`); check `audio/` and voice box before promising anything.
- What David must provide: (a) a 30-60 s clean reference recording of a woman who agrees in writing to the use (a family member or friend), or (b) approval of a stock TTS voice with a licence that allows paid ads, and whether the marketplace requires telling viewers it is AI-generated.
- Test first: his cloned voice (available now, no dependency, capped energy since the reference is calm), cut A; cut B with the woman's voice when provided; pick by a blind panel plus his ear. Run script_lint, voice_doctor and voice_quality as usual; say audio was measured, not heard.
