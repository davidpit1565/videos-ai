# Ad plan: Super Ivy (UGC-ad marketplace), written 19.11.2026

Publish mode: AD (not an episode, no numbering, no channel promotion). Goal: downloads of the app. Nothing built, nothing published.
Sources (verified 19.11.2026): App Store listing id6759669515 (S1), superivy.com (S2), David's brief facts (S3).
Note on scores: they are judgements on the 200 views = 50 / 10,000 = 100 scale, not measurements. An ad's reach is bought or assigned by the marketplace, so the "views" part is only a proxy for how well the video holds a stranger.

## 1. TOPIC verdict
- Big problem: your hands are busy but the to-do list keeps growing (emails to answer, plans to add, things to remember), and typing it all into a phone is the chore.
- Why a stranger cares today: everyone has an inbox and a calendar; "just say it out loud" is understood in one second. App copy itself: "Your hands are busy. Your mind is not." (S1).
- Proof on screen: the real App Store icon and real screenshots, plus the app's own preview video, showing you speak and it drafts the email / adds the event.
- Weakness, said once: voice assistants already exist, so the difference is only "free, remembers you, imports memory from other AIs". Those three carry the ad.
- Funnel: LOW (direct response). The CTA is the point.

## Tone (David, 19.11.2026): confident BRAND voice
Direct, short, declarative, 100% confident, as a company that stands behind the app. No hedges ("I think", "maybe", "one of the"), no "I used it / I tried it / I built it / my app", no testimonials, no "best", no "private/secure". Confidence lives in wording and delivery, never in extra claims: every line still maps to a source below. Anything not in the sources ("never forget", "handles everything", "instantly") stays out.

## 6. Voice (confident brand voice needs confident, energetic delivery)
- What the tone means for the voice: short declarative lines read flat if the delivery is calm, so the voice must be bright, quick, and landing hard on the verbs ("talks", "drafts", "remembers"). David's clone is capped by its calm reference clip (said before), so it is the weakest fit for this tone.
- Recommendation: a woman's voice with an energetic, warm, assured read. Reason: Ivy is "she" (S1) and the ad is about talking to a voice. Judgement, no data.
- Not known: which other cloned/stock voices exist in the repo beyond David's profile (`audio/voice/profile/`); check `audio/` and voice box before promising.
- David must provide: (a) a 30-60 s clean reference of a woman who agrees in writing, ideally recorded in an excited, upbeat read (the clone copies the energy of the reference), or (b) approval of a stock TTS voice with a licence for paid ads, and whether the marketplace requires telling viewers a voice is AI.
- Test first: cut A with his clone at higher exaggeration (about 0.78, hook higher, accent-flagged lines lower), cut B with the woman's voice when provided; choose by blind panel plus his ear. Run script_lint, voice_doctor, voice_quality; audio is measured, not heard.
