## B. TOPIC verdict
- Problem: every time you try a new AI you start by explaining who you are and what you do again, and the AI you already use knows you better.
- Why a stranger cares: ChatGPT is the most familiar AI; "my AI knows me" is a felt asset, and the idea of carrying it over is new and in plain words.
- Proof on screen: the maker's own statements and the real icon/memory screenshots. Honest limit: the central "how" has no on-screen proof until David tests the import.
- What the story is worth: a distinct angle (no other voice assistant in our sources states it), aimed at ChatGPT users, but it rests on one line of text per source. Worth a test, not a sure thing.
- Funnel: LOW, direct response.
