This agent can send emails by itself. It never does.

Because once it's sent, you can't take it back — even if the tone is wrong or the fact
is wrong. One bad send and it's already out.

So it only ever writes a draft. You read it, you click send. One switch in the
workflow: create draft, never send.

Every agent that touches your inbox should stop right there — build the draft, never
the send. You click every time.
