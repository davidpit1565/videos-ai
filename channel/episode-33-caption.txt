By default, n8n never tells you when a workflow dies. A node fails, the execution turns red in your history — and that's it. No email, no Slack message, nothing.

Most people never open that history unless something already feels wrong. By then, it could've been broken for weeks. The fix is one setting: an Error Workflow, wired to an Error Trigger node.

Set it once and every future failure tells you immediately. It's not a hidden feature — n8n even ships their own template for it. Almost nobody turns it on.
