Anything you type to Instagram's AI chat can come back as an ad.

Since late 2025, Meta — the company behind Instagram — uses your chats with its AI to choose the ads and posts you see, the same way it uses a like or a search. Meta says sensitive topics like religion or politics stay out of ads.

The catch: there's no off switch. Reports say Europe and the UK are left out, but most places aren't.

What you can do: if you use the Meta AI app, you can delete your chats — Menu, Settings, Data and privacy, Manage your information, Delete all chats. Meta doesn't say whether that changes your ads, so think before you type. Treat it like a search box, not a diary.
