Is that Amazon sale real? Check what it cost last month!

The exact steps:
1. Open any product on Amazon (U.S., UK, Canada or India site).
2. Click the price history link, right next to the price.
3. Look at the 30, 90 or 365 days. Amazon says the 365-day view is still rolling out in the U.S., UK and India.
4. Compare today's price with those weeks. That comparison is your own judgement, Amazon's page doesn't say whether a deal is real.

The extra trick (price alert): Amazon's guide says you can ask Alexa for Shopping (the assistant that was called Rufus until 13 May 2026) to set an alert. Amazon's own examples: "Set a price alert for when this face cream is $75." and "Has this item been on sale in the past 30 days?" Amazon says it will regularly check prices and alert you when the item reaches your price target. Amazon's page says all U.S. customers have access to Alexa for Shopping.

It can also buy for you at your target (example: "Buy these headphones when they're 30% off"), using your default payment method. The pages we read don't mention a spending limit or a confirmation step, so think before you turn that one on.

Keep in mind: this video shows where Amazon's own price history is. It doesn't test whether any particular deal is real. The chart in the video is a drawn example, not a real price. Not confirmed by us: the exact name of the link on your screen, and where the Alexa for Shopping button sits in the app.
