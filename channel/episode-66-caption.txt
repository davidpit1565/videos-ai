Is that Amazon sale price real?

Amazon's own help pages say its shopping assistant (called Rufus until 13 May 2026, now Alexa for Shopping) can show 30, 90 and 365 days of price history. To find it: open any product and click the price history link, right next to the price. Price history is available to customers in the U.S., UK, Canada and India, and the 365-day view is still rolling out in the U.S., UK and India.

The same assistant can buy for you when a price reaches a number you set (Amazon's example: "Buy these headphones when they're 30% off"), using your default payment method. The pages we read don't mention a spending limit.

Keep in mind: this video shows where Amazon's own price history is. It doesn't test whether any particular deal is real.
