## Product verified live (8.10.2026)

- Google's own Gemini Apps Privacy Hub (support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961), page last
  updated 24.9.2026: "A subset of chats are reviewed by human reviewers"; chats are
  disconnected from your Google Account before going to service providers; "Reviewed chats are
  retained for up to three years"; deleting your activity does not remove chats already
  reviewed; turning off Keep Activity stops future chats from being reviewed.
- Chats made while Keep Activity is off (and Temporary Chats) are kept 72 hours "with your
  account", not used to train Google's models, and not reviewed unless you send feedback.
- Google's activity help page (support.google.com/gemini/answer/13278892), desktop steps:
  gemini.google.com > Settings & help > Activity > click **On** near the top > **Turn off**
  (or **Turn off and delete activity**). Auto-delete choices: 3, 18 or 36 months; default 18.
- **UNCONFIRMED / not said in the video:** the phone (Android/iPhone) steps — Google's page
  links a separate article that was not read. The video gives the website steps only.
- **UNCONFIRMED:** how many chats are picked ("a subset" — no number from Google).
- **UNCONFIRMED:** that the setting is on by default for every adult in every country
  (Notebookcheck only). Not claimed.

## Spoken script (plain language, ~60s)

1. Someone at Google can read your chats with Gemini, even after you delete them.
2. Gemini is Google's AI chat. Google says a small part of the chats get picked for real people to read.
3. Google cuts the chat off from your account, then keeps it for up to three years.
4. Here's the catch. Wiping your history does not remove a chat that was already picked.
5. You can stop it for new chats. On the Gemini website, open Settings, then Activity, click On, and choose Turn off.
6. Google still holds new chats for a few days, but says they're not read.  (Google says 72 hours; spoken "a few days" because the voice bursts on "three".)
7. Send this to someone who tells Gemini everything.
8. The setup's in the link in bio. (locked)
9. Tomorrow, I'll show you what happens to a ChatGPT chat after you delete it. Follow, so you get the full story.
