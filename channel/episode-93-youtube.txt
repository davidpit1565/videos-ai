Title: A teacher says AI wrote your essay. 3 things that prove you wrote it

Description:
Your teacher says an AI wrote your essay. You wrote it. 3 things that help:
1. Show your version history (Google Docs: File, Version history, See version history).
2. Show your notes and drafts.
3. Ask the tool how often it is wrong.

A Stanford study tested 7 other AI detectors on 91 essays by people still learning English: about 6 in 10 were flagged as AI. Turnitin was not part of that test.
Turnitin says its false positive rate is under 1% (March 2023). Vanderbilt's own estimate: about 750 of 75,000 papers could be wrongly labeled. Vanderbilt turned the detector off in August 2023.

The message at the start is an example, not a real conversation.
