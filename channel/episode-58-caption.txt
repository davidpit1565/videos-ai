That photo in your WhatsApp chat right now — type one sentence, and Meta's AI will redraw it for you, before you even hit send.

It's called Photo Touch-Up. It's already built into the chat box in WhatsApp (not a separate app), and it's free. Open a chat, pick a photo like you normally do, tap the new edit button, and type what you want changed — remove something, swap the background, change the whole mood. The AI rebuilds the photo to match, right there before you send it.

It rolled out worldwide in March 2026, right inside the WhatsApp you already have.

The honest limit: it still gets some details wrong, and it's not a real photo editor — but for a quick fix before you hit send, it gets the job done.
