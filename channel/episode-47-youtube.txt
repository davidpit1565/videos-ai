An AI agent already texts you before your forgotten trial turns into a real charge.

Rocket Money launched Rowan on August 25, 2026 — an AI agent that watches a user's connected accounts in the background and reaches out by text the moment it finds something: a free trial about to convert to a paid charge, a price increase, a duplicate subscription, a bill it can negotiate down. Reply in plain language and Rowan carries out the action — cancelling a subscription, negotiating a bill, or pursuing a refund — then confirms once it's done. A sourced example from the launch coverage: if you sign up for a free trial and never use it, Rowan texts you about three weeks later asking whether to cancel before the first real charge hits.

Rowan is not open to every user yet. It's a limited rollout inside Rocket Money's new Premium+ tier ($15/month), with a waitlist for everyone else and no announced date for a wider release. Rocket Money's older, free tier already shows every recurring subscription it finds, and the standard Premium tier (pay-what-you-think-is-fair, roughly $7-$14/month) already offers non-AI cancellation — Rowan is the new AI layer on top of both.
