Your phone can save a map pin inside every photo you take. Apple says the people you send a photo to may be able to see where it was taken.

OpenAI's own help page says ChatGPT does not process a photo's metadata, so the hidden location is not what it reads. This video is built from those two official pages, not from a ChatGPT test: no screen recording, no answer from ChatGPT.

To send a photo without its location on iPhone: Photos > Share > Options > Location off.
