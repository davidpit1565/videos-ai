Title: Claude turns a boring report into a video (Claude Motion)

Description:
Claude Motion: according to Anthropic, Claude turns your report, data or images into a short animation, and you can change one word, number or timing without rebuilding it.
How, per Anthropic's help page: ask in a chat, or choose Output then Motion in the message box. Say who will watch and how long. Change one detail. Click Export and choose MP4.
We have NOT tested it. It is in beta on Team and Enterprise plans only. The animations in this video are drawn examples, not real Claude Motion output.
