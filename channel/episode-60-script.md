## Product verified live (7.10.2026)

- Meta's own announcement (about.fb.com, 2025/10, "Improving Your Recommendations on Our Apps
  With AI at Meta"): from 16.12.2025 Meta uses interactions with its AI (voice or text) to
  personalize content and ads; no full opt-out ("Ads Preferences and other feed controls" can
  adjust ads/content); sensitive topics (religious views, sexual orientation, political views,
  health, racial or ethnic origin, philosophical beliefs, trade union membership) are not used
  for ads; "rolling out in most regions".
- Meta's own help page (meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/2457110494637611): deleting chats
  in the **Meta AI app** = Menu (top left) > Settings (bottom left) > Data & privacy > Manage
  your information > Delete all chats and media > Delete all. The page gives no steps for chats
  inside Instagram/Facebook/Messenger/WhatsApp.
- **UNCONFIRMED / said carefully:** which countries are excluded. Tom's Guide, PPC Land and
  others report EU, UK and South Korea are left out; Meta's own page only says "most regions".
  The video says "Reports say Europe and the UK are left out. Most places aren't." and does not
  say South Korea (the word's final TH is swallowed by this voice; the site page lists it).
  David is in Belgium (EU): not affected by his own account.
- **UNCONFIRMED:** whether deleting chats changes ads. Meta does not say. The video says so.
- **UNCONFIRMED:** that nothing changed between 12.2025 and 10.2026 — no newer Meta post on AI
  chats and ads was found; a June 2026 Meta post concerns off-Meta activity, a different
  control. Not tested on a live account.

## Spoken script (plain language, ~60s)

1. Anything you type to Instagram's AI chat can come back as an ad.
2. Since late 2025, Meta — the company behind Instagram — uses your chats with its AI to pick your ads and posts.
3. Ask about a trip, and trip ads can follow. Meta says sensitive topics, like religion or politics, stay out of ads.
4. Here's the catch. There's no off switch. Reports say Europe and the UK are left out. Most places aren't.
5. If you use the Meta AI app, you can wipe your chats: Menu, Settings, Data and privacy, then Delete all chats.
6. Meta doesn't say if that changes your ads. So pause, then type. Treat it like a search box.
7. Send this to someone who chats with Instagram's AI.
8. The setup's in the link in bio. (locked)
