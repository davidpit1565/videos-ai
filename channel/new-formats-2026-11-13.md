# New formats from outside evidence (research, 13.11.2026)

Brief: formats about AI that get very large views from SMALL accounts, not "chat odd test", not news explainers (David, 13.11.2026). Method: Exa/web search, yt-dlp metadata on TikTok (view counts read live on 13.11.2026), a WebFetch of an Invidious channel page. Instagram itself was not scraped. Nothing was downloaded into the project. Every number below is "as publicly shown" on the date it was read; NOT AVAILABLE means I could not see it.

Honest summary: only ONE format clears the bar "at least two real examples with numbers I could read" (Format 1). Format 2 has one creator only. The rest are listed with what is missing. Fewer than the 5-8 asked for, because I did not want to pad with formats whose numbers I could not see.

## Format 1: "One prompt, four AIs" (side-by-side contest)

Template: one exact prompt, pasted unchanged into four chatbots; the four real outputs (an animation or a small app that runs) play in a 2x2 grid with the logos; a verdict. Slot that changes: the prompt (and which four AIs).

Examples (numbers as shown):
- AI Code Clash, YouTube Shorts channel, 10K subscribers (as shown on the Invidious mirror, `https://invidious.tiekoetter.com/channel/UCHGFRElhzCWc2xLS3zfJ5HQ/shorts`, read 13.11.2026). 42 Shorts listed. Biggest: "Fractal Tree Animation (p5.js)" 14M; "Multi Spring Wave Simulation" 780K; "Rain & Umbrella Simulation" 683K; "Futuristic Energy Beam City" 495K (also on `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aNnxn9XvdY0`); "Melting Glacier" 330K; "Car Crash" 311K (`https://www.youtube.com/shorts/WMoxjShCSB0`). Most others 6K to 100K, several under 10K. Dates: NOT AVAILABLE (the mirror shows a bogus "56 years ago"). The list was read by WebFetch's small model, so spot-check the biggest numbers on YouTube before quoting them.
- @ouali.code on TikTok (followers NOT AVAILABLE). Read with yt-dlp 13.11.2026: "ChatGPT vs Claude vs DeepSeek vs Gemini || DNA helix" 303,100 views, 7,560 likes, 431 reposts, 2.10.2026 (`https://www.tiktok.com/@ouali.code/video/7692106562906688788`); "Geometric Atom" 87,400, 9.10.2026 (`.../video/7694711718798232852`). The same series' other 9 recent posts: 231 to 11,100.
- Also a third channel in the niche: "Code Battle AI" (`https://www.youtube.com/shorts/1P3XmjZGdd0`, numbers NOT AVAILABLE).

Why it spreads:
- FACT (our reading of the numbers): the winners are visual outputs that move (fractal tree, rain on umbrella, black hole). Hit rate is low and uneven: ouali.code 2 of 12 recent posts above 80K; AI Code Clash about 8 of 42 above 100K by the fetched list.
- HYPOTHESIS: the picture is alive for free (the outputs are animations), the viewer can pick a favourite and argue in comments, and the viewer can paste the prompt and check. No face needed.
- HYPOTHESIS: one video in Wifi Uni's tutorial (`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DnUgBL9KrDc`) claims the niche has a single account; I found at least three, so supply is already growing (his claim, not verified, and he is selling a community).

Faceless and honest for us: yes. We run the prompt ourselves on free plans of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Meta AI (Meta AI producing a runnable page is UNVERIFIED), screen-record each real output, never retry silently (one run per AI, say so; if a retry happens, show it). Real logos on screen per the standing rule. 15 to 25 s fits: 3 s prompt on screen, 4 outputs, verdict. Risk: free-plan model names change fast, verify live before each script; outputs depend on the plan and day, say which model and plan.

Duplicate check: `topics-covered.txt` has nothing on side-by-side contests (only #63 "one company ranked 13 AI chats" for privacy). `content-memory.md` has no side-by-side or same-prompt entry (grep: no hit). Not a duplicate. It also is not a chat odd test: no trick played on the AI, the output is the content.

## Format 2: Numbered "one thing" series

Template: fixed title card "Things you didn't know you can do with AI (part N)", one real screen recording of the thing working, same layout every time. Slot: the thing.
Examples: @practicalyai (TikTok, 116.2K followers as shown on its profile page; also posts as @digitalsamaritan). yt-dlp 13.11.2026: part 258, 217,500 views, 30.9.2026 (`https://www.tiktok.com/@practicalyai/video/7691478400166399252`); part 54, 174,900 views, 4.12.2024 (`https://www.tiktok.com/@digitalsamaritan/video/7444575162642615607`); recent parts 279/280: 2,597 / 3,468; Claude part 70: 10,400. A search snippet showed part 54 at 648,251; the yt-dlp number is the one I trust.
Why it spreads: FACT: 280 installments prove the template is repeatable; typical recent parts are 2K to 10K with rare 170-220K hits (so most installments do not travel). HYPOTHESIS: a fixed container lowers decision cost for the viewer and for us.
Weakness: one creator, not a small account; closest to the tips/explainer class David is tired of. Use only as the NUMBERING wrapper for real tests, not as its own bet.
Duplicate check: tips on ChatGPT settings are covered (episodes 70, 71, 73, 81, 84); pick tricks outside settings.
