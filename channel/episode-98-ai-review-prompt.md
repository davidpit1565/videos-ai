# Prompt to paste into another AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Meta AI, Perplexity) — episode 98 red-team review
(Everything below the line is the prompt. Facts are real and must not be changed by the reviewer.)
---
You are a ruthless short-video strategist. I am about to publish an Instagram Reel (faceless, English, about 25 seconds, vertical 1080x1920) on a small channel about AI. My goal is a very large reach. Your job is NOT to praise it. Find every problem you can, force-rank them, and tell me exactly what to change so it has the best chance to go viral. Do not invent facts about the video and do not change the real results.

WHAT THE VIDEO IS (plain words)
I asked five AI chatbots the same thing: "write the instructions that steer a car around a race track." Each AI's answer was put, untouched, into its own car in a simple top-down race game, and the five cars raced two laps on the same road. I ran the race myself with a track and rules I locked before any AI answered. One try per AI, no second run, no fixing. No code is shown in the video.

THE REAL RESULT (run 3 times, identical)
1. Claude: finished 2 laps in 8.48 s
2. Gemini: finished in 8.68 s (0.20 s behind Claude)
3. ChatGPT: finished in 10.58 s
- Meta AI: touched the wall in the hairpin bend at 6.15 s
- Perplexity: got stuck in the hairpin (never finished)
Disclosure: the channel's builder is a Claude model, and Claude won. The race rules were locked before the answers existed.

HOW THE REEL IS BUILT
Look: hand-drawn paper style, light background, real brand logos on the five cars, a live ranking list with lap dots, a close-up card for the crash in the hairpin, a "SLOW MOTION" label for the finish, an end card with the result list. Captions/headline on screen, max 3 lines at a time. Music bed, energetic voice (a cloned calm voice, slightly excited).
Opening (frame 0): headline "FIVE AIs RACE, ONE HITS THE WALL." over the track with five logo cars at the start line.
Spoken script (about 25 s):
1. Five AIs race, one hits the wall.
2. Each AI gets the same job: teach a car to race.
3. Hands off. Go!
4. ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Meta AI, Perplexity.
5. Perplexity stops dead in the bend!
6. Meta AI hits the wall!
7. Claude and Gemini are neck and neck.
8. Claude pulls ahead!
9. ChatGPT takes its time.
10. Claude wins. Gemini is two tenths behind.
11. Send it to a friend. See who they pick.
12. The setup's in the link in bio.
13. Tomorrow, they face a maze. Follow, so you see who gets lost.
Caption (short): explains the same request, one try each, results, the disclosure that a Claude model made the video, and asks viewers to comment which AI they expected to win.
It will be posted first as an Instagram "trial reel" (shown only to non-followers), then moved to everyone after 3 days.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES (my own honest list)
- The winner is revealed fairly late and the ranking bar shows Claude leading early, which may spoil the end.
- The race is only about 10 seconds of real time; the rest is setup and reveal.
- Two of the five cars never finish, which is good drama, but the viewer may not feel why each failed.
- The cloned voice is calm; the energy is limited.
- The format is new for the channel (not proven). Related formats by other small channels (same prompt, rival AIs, real output) have reached hundreds of thousands to millions of views on YouTube/TikTok; I have no readable numbers for this exact hybrid.

WHAT I WANT FROM YOU (answer in this order, plain words, no code talk)
1. In one sentence: what is this video about? Would you stop scrolling in the first 2 seconds? Exactly why or why not?
2. The 10 biggest problems, ranked from most to least damaging, each with one sentence of why it hurts reach (retention, shares, comments, follows).
3. What most likely stops this from going viral, and what is the one change that matters most?
4. Fixes: for the first 3 seconds (visual + spoken line), the middle, and the last 3 seconds. Give exact wording and exact visuals, not advice like "make it punchier".
5. Five alternative spoken opening lines (max 7 words, not a question, true whatever the order), and your pick.
6. A mechanic that makes people comment and share (a reason to guess, a named person to send it to, a reason to watch twice) that stays honest.
7. What to cut or shorten so every second earns its place.
8. Honest scores 0-100 for HOOK, VIRALITY, VALUE and OVERALL, with one line of reasoning each, and a realistic range of views for a small account (say clearly it is a guess).
Rules: never invent claims about the results; keep the real numbers; no fake statistics; if you are unsure about a platform fact, say so.
