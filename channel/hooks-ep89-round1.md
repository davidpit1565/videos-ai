## My ranking, top 8 (judgement, not measurement)

1. Number 4, "Your barber asks. ChatGPT just cuts." Everyone has had a bad haircut, so the problem is felt in the body, it is funny, the picture (scissors) moves, and it is sendable to "the friend who uses ChatGPT for everything".
2. Number 1, "ChatGPT guesses. Make it ask first." Subject is word 1, the threat is word 2, and the fix is promised in six words; clearest comprehension of the set.
3. Number 14, "Don't answer yet, ChatGPT. Ask me." Same talk-to-the-AI shape as our best reel (86), but the subject is said out loud; a human bossing the AI is a small power fantasy.
4. Number 5, "Wrong answer? ChatGPT never asked you." Names something that already happened to the viewer and moves the blame onto a gap they can fix.
5. Number 15, "ChatGPT isn't dumb. It's guessing." Contradicts what many viewers already think ("it's dumb"), which is a reason to argue in the comments.
6. Number 13, "Ten words stop ChatGPT guessing." A checkable number, and the frame-0 picture is the save-worthy thing itself.
7. Number 10, "A doctor asks first. ChatGPT doesn't." Raises the stakes beyond a haircut; risk: it can read as a health claim, so the picture must stay on the comparison.
8. Number 7, "One line makes ChatGPT interview you." Role reversal is an unusual picture (the mic turning to the viewer); weaker on felt stakes.

Notes for the build: 4, 10, 24 and 29 are one family (pick one). Hooks 8 and 21 depend on the live OpenAI Academy page. 25 needs two real runs of ours. None of the payoffs may say the answer "is better" unless we ran it; "it asks first" is the claim we can show.
