### What it does (measured)
- One continuous voice-over, 206 words in 54.5 s = 3.8 words/s, -14.1 LUFS, loudness range 2 LU, silence only in the last 0.7 s. Captions at the TOP of the frame, 1-4 words at a time, the current key word lit lime-yellow, earlier words fading grey.
- Still share 18.2% (our ep81 was 50%, our v6 reels 5-8%); longest still run 0.5 s; only one hard cut (14.07 s); scenes change by 3D camera tilt/zoom/fly-through, never wipes; about 4.5 composition changes per 10 s.
- Visual language: dark background, white heavy sans, lime-yellow key word, real GitHub UI and logo, a drawn fake app ("Habits"), a drawn terminal, 3D laptop renders, the creator's profile card at the end. This conflicts with our light + lime base: copy the STRUCTURE only.
- Script (faster-whisper): hook question at 0-4.1 s ("What actually is GitHub, and why does everyone building with AI keep mentioning it?"); one-line answer; identity line at ~10 s ("If you learn to build with AI, nobody showed you."); first real payoff ("tell Claude to go back to the last commit that worked") at ~21-24 s (about 40-44% in); then micro-beats of 3-4 s, one concept each (push, clone, branch, merge, tag), all under one metaphor (a timeline); ends on a sayable instruction ("tell Claude to commit and push") and the follow reason "Follow for more vibe coding essentials". No loop, no comment-keyword CTA, no named person to send to.
- Claim check: GitHub/Git as described matches GitHub's own docs [agent, sources in its notes]. NOT to be copied: "a habit over 50 years old / 1972" (Git is from 2005; only version control in general is older) and the on-screen "96.65% of 53,374 developers" survey card (not verified).
