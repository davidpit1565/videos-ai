Three things your AI agent still breaks on.

It forgets everything between sessions. It gets stuck the moment a site asks you to log
in. And it can send an email you never meant to send — with no undo.

Three fixes, one line each:

Memory: paste in what changed since last time — don't rely on it remembering.
Logins: tell it to stop and ask you. Never guess a password or click through one.
Undo: make it show you a draft first. You click send, not the agent.

None of these need a rebuild. They need you to say it once, in the instructions.
