3 ways to make ChatGPT stop agreeing with you

Your AI chat likes to agree with you. Three things to try: ask "What's wrong with this?" instead of "What do you think?", add a Custom instructions line (Personalization > Custom instructions) such as "don't agree with me just because I sound sure", and pick the Candid option in Personalization > Base style and tone.
