- Copied structure: a numbered series ("PROMPT #1"), ONE prompt, 7-9 s, a loop (ends on the first frame), too short to read once so people rewatch and screenshot, the prompts in the caption, comment word.
- Ours: our voice (a short spoken hook), our look (torn-paper collage base from ep88, temporary until the brand is decided), the real OpenAI source named on screen with the OpenAI logo, a stated reason in one line. No "banned", no income promise.
- Hook (surprise in the first 3 words, 4-5 words): "OpenAI says: paste this first." Shown on frame 0 with the logo and the prompt card.
- Lines (present tense): 1 "OpenAI says: paste this first." 2 the prompt appears typed, read aloud. 3 "It makes ChatGPT ask, not guess." 4 "Save it. Send it to someone who types short prompts."
- Caption: the prompt, the source link, 2 more prompts from the same OpenAI page (Step 2 and Step 3), comment word proposal "PROMPT" (no auto-DM exists: manual).
