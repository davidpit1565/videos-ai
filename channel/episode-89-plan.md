Rule applied (coordinator, David): OVERALL must be at least 85 from the blind panel, else no full script ("we want virality, not a video"). Scores are judgements.

## What I tried
Structure: C1 LADDER (@ekkobuild) with our own measured test, because it is the only one with a result of ours. Topic: "A screenshot can erase the 'Made with AI' label" (not ChatGPT, drawn UI, no recordings). Ladder = 4 levels (re-save, shrink, PNG, converter): label YES -> GONE, then twist (Google's invisible SynthID watermark is built to survive crops/filters/compression) and a SAVE card "no label never means real: 3 checks".
Rejected C2 LIST ("6 apps your iPhone replaces"): iPhone-only, drawn results, rated about the same in round 2 (OVERALL 82 by judgement).

## Why it falls short
Abstract (a label, metadata), no felt personal stake, our test is a lab sample file (not a real AI image, not the real apps), and a still image with a line promises nothing to keep watching. What would lift it: a real, famous fake photo the viewer already saw, shown with the label vanishing in the real app (needs a screen recording, against David's constraint), or a personal stake (scam/family) instead of a label.
