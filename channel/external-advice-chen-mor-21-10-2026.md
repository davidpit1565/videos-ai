## The framework he teaches (his claims, summarised)
1. Pick a universal topic that speaks to a very large audience (his example: Thailand, for Israelis).
2. Do not chase "cracking the algorithm": it only boosts a video that already shows movement early, so build the video to get movement at the start.
3. A hook alone is not enough: the rest must pull the viewer to the end and trigger an action (like, comment, share).
4. Evoke an emotion and keep it alive the whole way. His main example is curiosity: show something and do NOT say what or where it is until the end.
5. Make script, picture and voice match each other, and keep it very fast (the platforms are impatient).
6. A sound effect is optional; a background tune that fits the message is almost always worth it.
