Your phone hides a map pin inside your photos (iPhone privacy)

Apple says that when Location Services is on for the Camera, the coordinates where a photo was taken are saved inside it, and the people you share it with may be able to see them. OpenAI's help page says ChatGPT does not process a photo's metadata. No ChatGPT test and no screen recording in this one: it is built from those two official pages.

To send a photo without its location on iPhone: Photos > Share > Options > Location off.
