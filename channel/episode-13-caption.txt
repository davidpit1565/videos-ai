Your Instagram views might be wrong right now, and you'd never know it.

Picture one box. Instagram writes a number into it. A second later, TikTok writes its own
number into that exact same box — and TikTok's number erases Instagram's, completely. No
warning, no error. The number just looks normal.

This happens with Instagram and TikTok. It happens with Reels and Shorts. Any time two things
share one box instead of having their own.

The fix is simple: give each one its own box, never let them share.

If a number ever looks wrong and you don't know why — that's the first thing to check.
