If you track Instagram and TikTok views in the same tracker, one of them is probably losing right now — and you'd never know.

Here's the pattern: your automation pulls Instagram's numbers into a column, and looks
correct. Then it pulls TikTok's numbers into that exact same column — and quietly
overwrites Instagram's real number with TikTok's. No error. No warning. The column still
shows a normal-looking number. It's just the wrong platform's number now.

This happens the second two platforms feed the same field: Instagram + TikTok in one
tracker, Reels + Shorts merged into one "views" column, any multi-platform report built in
n8n, Zapier, Make, or a plain spreadsheet.

The fix is one rule, not a rebuild:

Give every platform its own column. Never let two platforms write to the same one.

If a number ever looks flat or wrong and you can't explain why — that's the first thing to
check.
