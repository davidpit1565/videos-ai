Claude keeps your files now — not just this one chat

It's called a Claude Project — upload your files and write your instructions once,
and every new chat you open inside that project already has your context. No
re-explaining what you're working on, no re-uploading the same files every time.
Same project, zero repeat work.

It's not unlimited. It won't share files with a project you didn't add them to —
each project holds its own set. The real cap is twenty files a project, five
megabytes each. It's not a chat you keep alive in the background either — you set
it up once, then reuse it every time you come back to that project.
