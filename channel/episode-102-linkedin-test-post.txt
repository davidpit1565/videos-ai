$780

That is what five apps cost a year, if you pay the cheapest paid plan of each. One person. No extras.

Here is every line item, from the pricing pages, nothing rounded:

- Loom Business: $18 a month, $216 a year
- Notion Plus: $10 a month, $120 a year
- Intercom Essential: $19 a month on the yearly rate, $228 a year
- Wispr Flow Pro: $12 a month on the yearly rate, $144 a year
- ElevenLabs Starter: $6 a month, $72 a year

Total: $780.

Each one has a free, open-source twin: Cap, AppFlowy, Chatwoot, OpenWhispr and VoiceStudio.

The part that surprised me: the free twin that gets the most attention is only 7 months old. VoiceStudio went past 100,000 GitHub stars in that time.

Four of the five paid apps already have a free plan. So the $780 only applies if you pay today.

Software people used to flip houses. Now they flip subscriptions.
