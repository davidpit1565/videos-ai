Six apps you pay for have free alternatives.

The list, each one free and open source:

1. Loom → Cap (screen videos)
2. Notion → AppFlowy (notes, docs, AI)
3. Calendly → Cal.diy (booking page, personal use only)
4. Intercom → Chatwoot (customer chat, free if you host it yourself)
5. Wispr Flow → OpenWhispr (you talk, it types)
6. ElevenLabs → VoiceStudio (AI voices on your own computer, only 7 months old)

Honest part: we did not test these. They are alternatives, not full replacements, and some need your own server. Check each one before you cancel anything.

Added up, the cheapest paid plan of each of these six apps is about $75 a month per person (official pricing pages, November 2026). Most of them also have a free plan.

Links, licences and the steps for each one are in the link in bio.

Save the list, and send it to a friend who pays for Loom.
