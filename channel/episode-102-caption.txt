Stop paying $780 a year for these five apps. Free alternatives exist.

The list, each one free and open source:

1. Loom → Cap (screen videos)
2. Notion → AppFlowy (notes, docs, AI)
3. Intercom → Chatwoot (customer chat, free if you host it yourself)
4. Wispr Flow → OpenWhispr (you talk, it types)
5. ElevenLabs → VoiceStudio (AI voices on your own computer, only 7 months old)

Honest part: we did not test these. They are alternatives, not full replacements, and one needs your own server. Check each one before you cancel anything.

The $780: the cheapest paid plan of each of the five apps, per person, from their official pricing pages in November 2026, yearly: Loom $216, Notion $120, Intercom $228, Wispr Flow $144, ElevenLabs $72. Four of them also have a free plan, so this only applies if you pay.

Links, licences and the steps for each one are in the link in bio.
