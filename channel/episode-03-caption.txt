Your AI agent is already lying to you.

Not maliciously — it just doesn't know how to say "I'm not sure." So when it doesn't
know, it guesses, states the guess with full confidence, and moves on. You only find
out later, when the guess was wrong.

Five real ways this shows up, from agents actually running in production right now.

The fix costs one line in your system prompt:

"If you're not sure, say so — instead of guessing."

That's it. Add it before you trust the output of anything you've built.
