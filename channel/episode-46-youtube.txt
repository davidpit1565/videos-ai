Your Copilot chats at work don't vanish when you close the tab.

Microsoft's own privacy page states that interacting with Copilot stores your prompts and its responses as your activity history — viewable and deletable by you from myaccount.microsoft.com → Privacy → Data and privacy → Copilot activity history.

Separately, Microsoft's Purview documentation describes how organizations can retain that same Copilot chat data under their own retention policies — Microsoft states retention can run up to about a year, or indefinitely, depending on the organization's license and configuration. Under a formal compliance or legal process (eDiscovery), someone holding the right Purview permission can read the actual prompt and response content — but Microsoft's own documentation states this requires specific permissions beyond general admin or manager status, not something every IT person or manager can do by default.

This is not live monitoring, and deleting your own activity history does not necessarily remove your organization's separately-retained copy.
