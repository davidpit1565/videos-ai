## Why it may spread (HYPOTHESIS, not tested by us)
- A finite numbered list that promises value (saves). A hook with a big, checkable money claim stated plainly.
- Real brand logos on both sides of every item make each beat instantly readable without sound.
- Repetition of one template = predictable rhythm; new information arrives every 4-7 s.
- A comment-keyword call to action (the creator probably uses a DM automation: not verified; we have no such tool).

## What is relevant for us
- It is the closest example we have found of OUR direction: faceless, light base with one accent, real logos, a fixed template per item, smooth motion. It supports the fixed series identity decision (fixed layout and badge system, only logos and names change per item), and the rule that every product named gets its real logo.
- Directly reusable ideas (own design, never copied artwork): a numbered badge system, a "replaces X" structure with a cancelled-logo to free-logo arrow, a progress indicator, a screenshot card with a cursor, the typed-comment end card.
- Risks/limits: the claim "replace thousands of dollars of paid software" needs the same live fact check as everything (what each repo really does, licences, whether it is really free/self-hostable); the comment-keyword funnel is a different growth method and needs its own tool; do not copy its repo list.
