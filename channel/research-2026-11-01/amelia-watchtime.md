## What she says (her words, condensed)
1. Watch time is how long you keep people's attention. Where the retention graph dips is where attention dips; it tells you exactly where to change the video.
2. Her three steps for a video that held attention:
   - Hook: interesting to a wide group, triggers one specific emotion, and OPENS A LOOP that leaves the viewer in suspense ("what is she going to do with that?"). Proof of authority helps but is not needed in every hook.
   - A valuable hack that is easy and quick to do, giving real, immediate results for one specific problem.
   - A REHOOK at the end that offers additional value in the caption, so people keep watching and engaging with the post.

## How it maps to us (our own read)

Ours: ep 81 'ChatGPT is a freaking yes-man!' has broad interest and attitude but NO open loop; ep 80 'ChatGPT can cave when you push!' neither a loop.
