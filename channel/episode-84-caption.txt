ChatGPT memory: does deleting a chat delete the notes?

OpenAI's own help page says saved memories are stored separately from your chats. Deleting a chat alone does not necessarily delete a memory made from it. To remove one, delete the saved memory and the chat where you first shared it.

The path: Settings, Personalization, Memory. If you do not see a setting, it is not available for your account yet.

Said plainly: this shows what OpenAI's page says and where to look. It does not claim what ChatGPT saved about you.
