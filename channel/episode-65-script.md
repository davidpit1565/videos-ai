## Product / claims verified live (9.10.2026)

First-party: Google's Gmail Help, "Collaborate with Gemini in Gmail"
(support.google.com/mail/answer/14199860):
- Gemini can "Create new emails or refine existing drafts for tone and clarity" — you type a prompt and click **Submit**.
- The page does not say Gemini sends emails on its own.
- It "requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan"; personal accounts can try it only through
  Google Workspace Experiments (a trusted-tester program). The page does not say free personal accounts are eligible.
- Where it is: **Ask Gemini**, top right of Gmail.
- Actions that change emails are not applied until you confirm; then there are 60 seconds to undo.
- Limits stated: requests matching more than 10,000 threads are not processed; it can only add labels that already exist.

Third-party only (NOT to be said as fact): Smart Reply/Smart Compose are free for everyone; "Help me reply" button;
ChatGPT's Gmail connector (paid plan, send needs approval each time, reportedly unavailable in the EU/UK).
