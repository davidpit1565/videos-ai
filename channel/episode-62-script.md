## Product verified live (8.10.2026)

- OpenAI help center, "Deleting and archiving chats in ChatGPT" (help.openai.com/en/articles/8809935) and
  "Chat and File Retention Policies" (8983778), as returned by search snippets: a deleted chat is removed
  from your account immediately and scheduled for permanent deletion from OpenAI systems within 30 days,
  unless it was already de-identified or OpenAI must keep it longer for security or legal reasons. Temporary
  Chats are auto-deleted within 30 days. Deleting a chat does not delete files saved to Library. The page
  itself notes retention "may be affected by recent legal developments".
- Press (Engadget 11.10.2025 and others): a May 2025 court order in the New York Times v. OpenAI case made
  OpenAI preserve output logs that would otherwise be deleted; a 9.10.2025 order ended that duty going
  forward (from 26.9.2025). Logs saved under the order stay accessible, and data on accounts the Times
  flagged is still kept.
- Steps (OpenAI help + guides, web): profile picture > Settings > Data controls > Delete all chats > confirm.
  Phone: menu > profile > Settings > Data controls.
- **UNCONFIRMED:** OpenAI's own page on the lawsuit (openai.com/index/response-to-nyt-data-demands/) returned
  403, so the court-order details come from press, not from OpenAI. The video says "Reports say".
- **UNCONFIRMED:** Whether the bulk delete also removes archived chats (a third-party guide says yes; OpenAI
  does not say). Not claimed.
- **UNCONFIRMED:** the exact label of the confirm button. Not claimed beyond "Delete all chats".
