## 1. Honest diagnosis: why the earlier rounds read as "not professional"

Judgement, from looking at `logo-v1/sheet.png`, `logo-v2/sheet.png`, `logo-kit-B/kit-overview.png`, `wordmark-v3/sheet.png`.

**The A + yellow swipe (kit B)**
1. It reads as a letter that was crossed out. A bar through a letter is the sign for "deleted" or "wrong", the opposite of "works".
2. The A is a font letter (Archivo Black with the crossbar removed). Removing the crossbar of an A is one of the most used logo moves (round 2 research already cited https://nicklevesque.medium.com/7-logo-letterforms-to-let-go-of-f38254273b72). Nothing in it was drawn.
3. On the light version the yellow swipe sits behind a black wedge on white: it looks like a sale sticker or a road sign. On the dark version the swipe cuts the letter into two pieces, and at 32 px the letter is no longer an A (visible in the kit's own x4 enlargements).
4. Yellow + black + white with hard diagonal cuts is the look of construction tape, tabloids and discount flyers. The colour pair alone is not the problem (Vox uses it); the slanted bar is.
5. Weak silhouette in the circle: a narrow triangle plus a bar that pokes out sideways leaves most of the disc empty and off balance.
6. It says nothing about AI, testing, or "works", and it has no charm: no face, no object, no joke. The accounts David likes are all faces.
7. The idea is thin: one letter + one stripe. A viewer cannot describe it in a way that is ours ("the A with a line").

**v1 (check card / aw / switch / stamp):** stock interface icons and a default check mark; a check in a circle next to a username looks like a fake verified badge; "aw" is a sound of pity.

**Wordmark with WORKS on a yellow block:** two stacked words with the second on a filled colour block is the construction of the Pornhub logo. Standing rule now: no word inside a filled box.

**What all three rounds shared:** they started from a font letter or a UI icon and decorated it, on a black disc. None started from a silhouette of its own.

## 4. The four finalists

Files per finalist `N-name-…`: `avatar-dark.svg` / `avatar-light.svg` (full square, for upload), `avatar-*-1024.png` (square, the subject sits inside Instagram's circle), `small-*.svg` (the simplified drawing used at 40 px and below, circle-clipped), `favicon-64-*.png`, `wordmark-*.svg/png` (text converted to outlines, no box around any word). "dark" = black/white/yellow system on ink `#0B0B0C`; "light" = the colour version (cobalt `#2447FF`, yellow `#FFE600`, white, ink; the button uses red `#FF2D2D`).

1. **A-character.** The capital A drawn as a small character: rounded apex, three zig-zag feet, two big eyes, one raised eyebrow. Small sizes drop the eyebrow and enlarge the eyes. Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold.
   - Looks designed: yes, one silhouette, one stroke logic. Ownable: the most of the four (letter + character). 32 px: holds (yellow wedge with two dots).
2. **Raised eyebrow.** Two round eyes, the left one half closed under a flat brow, the right brow raised: the look of someone who tests before believing. Wordmark: Archivo ExtraBold, width 112.
   - Looks designed: yes, strict circles and one brow weight. Ownable: medium (see look-alikes). 32 px: the strongest of the four.
3. **Plug.** A power plug as a face, prongs up, a smirk, the cord leaving the circle. Wordmark: Fredoka Bold.
   - Looks designed: fairly. Ownable: medium. 32 px: holds, but reads as a bunny. Risk: childish.
4. **Button.** One big red push button. The only finalist without a face. Wordmark: Unbounded ExtraBold.
   - Looks designed: clean, but it is an object anyone can draw. Ownable: lowest. 32 px: a red dome, clear.

Typefaces: all four are SIL Open Font License 1.1, taken from the `ofl/` folder of https://github.com/google/fonts (bricolagegrotesque, archivo, fredoka, unbounded). The font files are not in this folder; the wordmark SVGs are outlines.

## 5. Look-alike findings (web text search only, plus my own knowledge where marked)

- **A-character:** letters turned into ghost characters are a known genre on logo marketplaces (R, B, W examples: https://scalebranding.com/product/422730 , https://scalebranding.com/product/419803 , https://scalebranding.com/product/422162 ; an "A monster" with one eye: https://scalebranding.com/product/432842 ; a set of flat ghost mascots: https://inspiration.vasa.works/item/branding/daymzqf-mascot-logo-collection ). Zocdoc's "Zee" is a letter Z with two eyes on yellow: same idea, different letter https://medcitynews.com/2016/02/zocdoc-rebrands-zee-mascot/ . I found no existing brand that is a triangular A with zig-zag feet and eyes. From my own knowledge, UNVERIFIED here: the zig-zag hem recalls the Pac-Man ghosts and the Snapchat ghost (yellow field), so do not put it in white on yellow.
- **Raised eyebrow:** eyes as a logo exist (Animade by Koto, 2020: https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/animade-rebrand-koto-graphic-design-220920 ; Goker: https://goker.me/goker-logo-animation ), and "cartoon eyes with one raised eyebrow" is sold as stock art (https://www.vecteezy.com/vector-art/56618105-cartoon-eyes-expressing-confusion-and-suspicion-featuring-one-eye-with-a-raised-eyebrow-capturing-emotions-of-doubt-uncertainty-and-skepticism-in-a-playful-manner ). From my own knowledge, UNVERIFIED here: the "face with raised eyebrow" emoji and the Tripadvisor owl (two round eyes). This is the main weakness of finalist 2.
- **Plug:** electric mascots are old and famous in the US: Reddy Kilowatt (owned by Xcel Energy) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reddy_Kilowatt and Willie Wiredhand, whose legs are a two-prong plug https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Wiredhand . Both are full stick figures, not a plug head. From my own knowledge, UNVERIFIED: at small size it can recall the Android robot head or a rabbit.
- **Button:** Staples "Easy Button" (red dome button, word "easy" on it) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staples_Easy_Button ; Big Red Button Entertainment (a game studio whose logo is a red button) https://www.avid.wiki/Big_Red_Button_Entertainment ; BBC Red Button (a text logo) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_Red_Button . The object itself is generic.
- Palette: cobalt + yellow is also IKEA's colour pair (my own knowledge, UNVERIFIED here; their logo is an oval wordmark, nothing like these marks).

## 6. My ranking and pick

1. **A-character, colour version (yellow on cobalt)**: my pick. It is the only one that is both the name's letter and a face; the cobalt disc stays visible in Instagram's dark and light mode (the ink disc disappears on a black page, see the overview); it can react inside the videos (eyebrow up = "really?", eyes wide = "it works"); I found no near twin.
2. **Raised eyebrow (ink + yellow brows)**: the strongest at 32 px and the most expression, and it keeps the black/white/yellow palette. Second because it sits close to an emoji and to stock art.
3. **Button**: clean and the only non-face, for the case David does not want a character. Says "works", says nothing about AI, lowest ownability.
4. **Plug**: charming, but the most childish, and David has used "childish" as a rejection before.

Honest limit: David preferred the letter mark over the wink face in round 2, so a character may not be his taste. If none of the four lands, the useful answer from him is "character or no character".
