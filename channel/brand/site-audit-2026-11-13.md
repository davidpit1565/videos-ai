## The 5 changes that matter most for a visitor coming from a reel

1. **Stop sending lost visitors to a Hebrew password page, and make the crash screen English.** `middleware.ts:18-33`, `app/unlock/page.tsx:19-20`, `app/error.tsx:59-66`, `app/global-error.tsx:54-68`; add `app/not-found.tsx`.
2. **Put the reel on the first screen of the phone and give one clear action.** Today the first screen is header, "AI CREATOR", headline, numbers; the video comes after (`app/page.tsx:74-150`). Nothing on the site says "Follow @actually_works.ai" in words.
3. **Fix the header on a phone.** Five pills plus an icon row before any content, small tap targets, the studio's `nav` rule leaking in, and an unstyled header on `/p` and `/s` (`app/sitenav.tsx:15-21`, `app/globals.css:91-93`, `:1056-1086`, `app/p/[slug]/page.tsx:22`). Not seen on a device: check at 375 px first.
4. **Stop printing the studio `notes` field on public episode pages** (`lib/site.ts:45`, `app/e/[n]/page.tsx:227-234`). The bio link sends every visitor to `/e/N` through `/latest`, so this is the most visited page.
5. **Strip the generated-page look and the stale copy:** gradients and glows, the eyebrow on every page, ten motion effects, count-up numbers (`app/globals.css:534-538`, `:557-559`, `:598`, `:617-621`, `:1165-1187`, `:1259-1263`), and the strings that are no longer true ("One setup a week", "it grows one episode a week", "Reply to any issue", "No email needed"). Remove the safe-zone section from the home page.
