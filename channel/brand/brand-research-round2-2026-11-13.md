### 2.2 What the professional versions have in common (my reading of the sources above)

1. **One stroke, drawn once, then frozen.** Not a new random brush every time. (Mel Robbins, GET RAW.)
2. **The highlight is a tool with a rule**, not decoration: it marks the one thing that matters. (onlyfy, inDrive, Mel Robbins.)
3. **Imperfect on purpose, but controlled.** Human edge, strict system. (inDrive: "strict set of rules"; Mel Robbins: "imperfection".)
4. **It scales by simplification.** inDrive rejected the expressive versions because they did not scale.
5. **It lives in the typography too**, not only next to it. (onlyfy glyphs, Studio Fnt.)

### 2.3 Findings from rendering the v2 draft and the refinements (MEASURED, with JUDGEMENT marked)

- **Yellow against white has almost no brightness contrast.** `#FFE600` on `#FFFFFF` is 1.27 : 1 (round 1); on the Miki paper `#ECEAE4` it is about 1.05 : 1 (computed by me). The swipe is separated from a white A or from paper **by hue only**. In greyscale the white A and the swipe still separate (the yellow renders as a light grey), but weakly. Consequence: the swipe must always touch Ink somewhere, and must be large.
- **"Letter shows through the swipe in black" on the dark version was tested and rejected.** It breaks the mark into a checkerboard of white, black and yellow pieces and is unreadable at 40 px. (Rows "B2 dark through" and "B1 dark through" in the test sheet.)
- **On black, swipe on top is the strongest version at 32 px** (row "B2 dark ontop"): white cap, yellow band, two white feet.
- **On paper, swipe behind the letter is the true highlighter** (ink over highlight, as on a real page) and the A stays a complete black A. But the yellow area shrinks to the two overshoots and the notch, so it is weaker as an avatar (row "B1 paper behind").
- JUDGEMENT: the v2 swipe is slightly thin for comment size (about 3.5-4.3 px at 32 px). 150 units (4.8 px at 32 px) reads better.
- JUDGEMENT: in v2 the A's feet reach 86% of the circle radius and a small triangle of counter shows above the swipe; at 32 px that triangle is a speck. Either show a clear counter or none.

### 2.4 Refinement notes for B

**The A**
- Flat-topped wedge with a notch, no crossbar (the swipe is the crossbar). Keep the flat cuts at apex and feet; they separate it from a typed A.
- Proportions on the 1000 canvas: height 530 (y 235 to 765), base width 576, apex width 150 (v2 had 180: a narrower apex reads more like a letter and less like a tent), leg width 190-196 measured horizontally.
- Notch apex at y 521, so the swipe's top edge covers it. Above the swipe the A is one solid cap; below it two feet. No speck.
- Feet at about 78% of the radius (v2: 86%), leaving air to the circle edge. Only the swipe is allowed further out (to about 86%).
- Optical corrections to check by eye when drawing the final: the A's visual weight sits low (triangle), so the whole group may need to move up 8-12 units; the flat apex may want 4-6 units of overshoot above the cap line when it sits next to the wordmark; legs flare slightly toward the base (170 to 196), which is intended, keep it.

**The swipe**
- Angle: rising to the right, **7 degrees** (v2 was about 6 in total). JUDGEMENT: under 4 it looks like a ruled bar, over 10 it becomes a strike-through.
- Thickness: 150 units = about 0.8 of the leg width. Long edges parallel.
- Ends: both cut at the same slant, 70 degrees, like the face of a chisel tip (the Stabilo tip is a chisel). Parallel slanted ends are what make it a marker stroke and not a rectangle or a brush.
- Overshoot: about 160 units past each leg. The right end slightly higher and slightly further out (the hand lifts off).
- Position: centre line through (500, 585), i.e. about two thirds down the letter, where a crossbar would be. Not through the middle: a bar through the middle of a glyph is a strike-through.
- Texture: **none in the avatar.** Two or three small irregularities on the ends only, in the large version only (variant B1 below), drawn once and never regenerated. No dry-brush streaks, no transparency, no multiply effect, no gradient: those are the stock "brush stroke" look and they vanish under 64 px anyway.
- Layer rule: **on black the swipe lies over the letter; on paper or white the letter lies over the swipe.** Stated once, applied everywhere.

**Keeping it ownable** (JUDGEMENT)
- The letter is not ownable; the swipe's exact shape and rule are. Fix five things and never vary them: 7 degrees, 70-degree chisel ends, 0.8 thickness, overshoot both sides, one swipe per frame.
- Differ from Mel Robbins on purpose: hers is a soft, hand-drawn brush line **under a phrase**; ours is a hard-edged chisel band **across one letter or one word**, with flat cut ends, always with heavy black caps. Never use a wavy or dry-brush stroke.
- Use the swipe for one meaning only: "this is the part that actually works". Not as underline, divider, button or background.

**Small-size version**
- Under 64 px: variant B2 (clean parallelogram), no irregular ends.
- Under 24 px (favicon 16): drop the notch entirely. Solid wedge + band. Test at 16 px before shipping; I did not render 16 px.

**Avoid**
- A red or orange swipe (strike-through, and orange is Claude). A swipe through the middle of the letter. Thin swipe. Rounded ends. Brush texture. Transparency. Shadow. A second yellow element in the same frame. Yellow text. A yellow that drifts toward amber. A ring around the A (Avengers).

### 2.5 The wordmark (ACTUALLY over WORKS on a yellow bar)

JUDGEMENT on the v2 wordmark, and what to change:
- Good: Archivo Black caps, two lines, the bar under the second word. It already reads as a headline.
- The bar in v2 is a plain rectangle rotated 1.5 degrees. Give it the same DNA as the mark: **70-degree chisel ends**, top and bottom edges parallel.
- Angle for text: **2 degrees**, not 7. At 7 degrees a bar cuts through horizontal letters. The shared signature between mark and wordmark is the end cut and the overshoot, not the angle. (The examples in 2.1 keep text highlights near-horizontal; that is my reading of their descriptions, I did not measure their artwork.)
- Bar height 1.25 x cap height, sitting a little low: from 0.15 cap height below the baseline to about 1.1 cap height. Overshoot 0.35 cap height on the left, 0.5 on the right.
- Which word: "WORKS" on the bar (as in v2) matches the meaning "the part that works". Round 1 suggested "Actually"; v2's choice is better. Keep it fixed.
- Tracking: -3 at 124 px in v2 is tight; check the LL and LY pairs by eye and kern them by hand in the final outlines.
- Convert the wordmark to outlines in the final SVG so it does not depend on the font file. Licence of Archivo Black: OFL per round 1.
- One-line version for narrow places: ACTUALLY WORKS on one line, bar under WORKS only.

## 4. The system

| Piece | Spec |
