## What Instagram itself says (checked 13.11.2026)

- **Name and handle are used for search.** Instagram's own post "Breaking Down How Instagram Search Works"
  (https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works): "**Use a fitting handle
  and profile name.** Search results are matched by text. Using an Instagram handle or profile name that's related
  to the content of your posts is your best bet for showing up in relevant searches."
- **Bio keywords and location:** same page: "**Include relevant keywords and locations in your bio.** ... Make
  sure your bio includes keywords about who you are and what your profile is about. If your account is
  location-specific, like for a small business, sharing your location in your bio can make it easier for people in
  your area to find you." Note: Instagram puts the location in the BIO; Dan puts it in the NAME field. And it is
  for location-specific accounts, which we are not.
- **Captions:** same page: "put keywords and hashtags in the caption, not the comments."
- **Name changes:** "Instagram: You can update your profile name twice every 14 days."
  (https://help.instagram.com/2994759677322122/). So a name test costs one of two changes per two weeks.
- **Name field length: UNCONFIRMED.** I found no Instagram page that states the limit. Third-party pages disagree
  (30 and 64 both appear). Observed fact: Dan's live name field is 43 characters, so the limit is above 30. The
  proposals below are 32 characters; the app will show the real limit when David types.
- **Bio length:** 150 characters is the widely reported limit; I did not find it on an Instagram-owned page in this
  check (a facebook.com answers page repeats it). The app's counter decides.
- So Dan's point "search words in the name field" is backed by Instagram for SEARCH matching. Nothing in these
  pages says it affects reach in Reels or Explore.
