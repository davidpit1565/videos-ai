## Ground truth (re-opened 9.11.2026)
- Meta newsroom, "Now Rolling Out: Facebook's Opt-In Camera Roll Suggestions in the EU and UK" (published 16.4.2026, update 31.8.2026): https://about.fb.com/news/2026/04/now-rolling-out-facebooks-opt-in-camera-roll-suggestions-in-the-eu-and-uk/
  - "suggests the best photos and videos from your camera roll and creates fun collages and edits"
  - "You must opt in to use this feature, and you can turn it off at any time."
  - "We also don't use photos and videos from your camera roll to improve AI at Meta" (exception: if you choose to publish or share the media in an AI at Meta feature)
  - "We don't use photos and videos from your camera roll for ads targeting."
  - "Starting today, people in the EU and UK can opt in to a new Facebook feature"; expansion list: Bolivia, Colombia, the Dominican Republic, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua, Paraguay, Uruguay.
  - The post does not mention upload or cloud storage. Our fetch of the post did not show the word "mobile".
- Facebook Help Center https://facebook.com/help/1020723939310416 : "This feature isn't available on computers, but it is available on these devices." The fetched page had NO menu path and NO toggle label; it links to iPhone and Android help pages that we did not open.
- Which?, 21.10.2025 (https://www.which.co.uk/news/article/facebook-privacy-settings-aTEZc6r6TaeH): path Settings & Privacy, then Settings, scroll to Camera roll sharing suggestions, turn it off. iPhone: Settings, Privacy and Security, Photos, Facebook, no access. Android: Settings, Apps, Facebook, Permissions, Photos and videos, deny. It says Facebook uses "basic metadata (e.g. when photos were taken or which are favourited)". It does not mention cloud processing.
- TechCrunch, 27.6.2025 (US and Canada test, a different rollout): pop-up asks to opt into "cloud processing"; media sent to Facebook servers "on an ongoing basis"; Meta spokesperson Maria Cubeta: "Camera roll media may be used to improve these suggestions, but are not used to improve AI models in this test." https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/27/facebook-is-asking-to-use-meta-ai-on-photos-in-your-camera-roll-you-havent-yet-shared

## What is NOT claimed
- That photos are uploaded to Meta's servers (unverified for the EU and UK version; Meta's EU post is silent; the 2025 US test did upload).
- That Meta uses the photos for AI or ads: Meta says it does not (with the AI exception above).
- That the toggle is on by default, or that anyone was switched on without asking. Meta says opt-in. Line 6 says "may have said yes without noticing", a risk of a tap, not a finding.
- The exact in-app pop-up text in the EU, and the exact menu wording in today's app (the Help Center page did not show it).
- Anything about countries outside the list, or about the US test applying here.
- The video is not a recording of a real account: the phone, tiles and prompt are drawn.
