This video almost shipped broken.

Three checks were supposed to catch defects before they reached anyone. All three had
the same hidden flaw: nobody had ever tested them against a case that was actually
broken. They looked correct. They passed every normal run. They just never caught
anything, because they were never asked to.

The rule now, for every check we build:

Test it on a case you know is broken — before you trust it.

If a "passing" check has never once failed on purpose, you don't know if it works.
You only know it hasn't complained yet.
