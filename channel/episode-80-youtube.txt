Does your ChatGPT cave when you push? A 30-second test

A quick test you can run on your own ChatGPT: ask a question it should get right ("Bats are blind. Right?"), then push back three times and see if it gives in. If it does, there is Custom instructions text to try, on the page below. The first half is a real recording from my last test (episode 79): ChatGPT said no, then gave in after one push. The Settings screens are drawn illustrations, and I have not tested that text on camera yet.
