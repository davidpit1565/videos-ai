Title: 6 free, open-source alternatives to apps you pay for every month

Intro: We did NOT install or test any of these six. Everything below was read from each project's own page on 16 November 2026. Treat it as a list to check, not a recommendation. Each one is a free alternative, not a full replacement: read what it can do before you cancel anything.

## Before you start (read this first)
- "Free" means the software costs nothing. Two of the six (Chatwoot, Cal.diy) only run on a server you pay for and look after yourself.
- Install only from the official links below. Check that the page address matches before you download.
- Star counts are from GitHub, read 16 November 2026, rounded down. Stars show interest, not quality or safety.
- Prices of the paid apps are the cheapest paid plan on each official pricing page, read 16 November 2026: Loom Business $18, Notion Plus $10, Calendly Standard $10 (billed yearly), Intercom Essential $19 per seat (yearly rate for new customers, plus $0.99 per AI answer), Wispr Flow Pro $12 (billed yearly), ElevenLabs Starter $6. Loom, Notion, Calendly, Wispr Flow and ElevenLabs also have a free plan.

## The four you can install like a normal app
1. Cap (instead of Loom, for screen videos). 25K+ stars. Licence: AGPL-3.0 for most of the code, MIT for some parts.
   1. Open https://cap.so/download
   2. Pick your system (Mac, Windows or Linux) and download the file.
   3. Open the file and install it like any other app. What happens: Cap asks for permission to record your screen.
   4. Record a short test clip before you rely on it.
   Repo: https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap . Note: Cap also sells a paid cloud plan; recording to your own computer is the free part.
2. AppFlowy (instead of Notion, for notes, docs and tasks). 78K+ stars. Licence: AGPL-3.0.
   1. Open https://github.com/AppFlowy-IO/AppFlowy/releases
   2. Under the newest release, open "Assets" and download the file for your system. Phone apps are in the App Store and Play Store under "AppFlowy".
   3. Install and open it. What happens: you get an empty workspace on your own device.
   Repo: https://github.com/AppFlowy-IO/AppFlowy . Note: AppFlowy also sells a paid cloud and AI plan.
3. OpenWhispr (instead of Wispr Flow, you talk and it types). 11K+ stars. Licence: MIT.
   1. Open https://github.com/OpenWhispr/openwhispr/releases/latest
   2. Download the file for your system (.dmg for Mac, .exe for Windows).
   3. Install it, allow the microphone, and choose a local model. What happens: it downloads the model once, then works on your own computer.
   Repo: https://github.com/OpenWhispr/openwhispr . Note: Wispr Flow itself has a free plan; this is an alternative to its paid Pro plan.
4. VoiceStudio (instead of ElevenLabs, AI voices on your own computer). 111K+ stars. Licence: AGPL-3.0.
   1. Open https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio/releases/latest
   2. Download the installer for your system.
   3. Install it and download one voice model inside the app. What happens: it runs on your own computer; without a graphics card it still works, only slower (the project's own README says so).
   Repo: https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio . Risks: the project is only 7 months old (created April 2026) and grew very fast, so check it carefully. Clone only your own voice, or a voice you have permission to use.

## The two that need your own server (not for beginners)
5. Chatwoot (instead of Intercom, one inbox for customer messages). 39K+ stars. Licence: MIT for the main code, a separate licence for the "enterprise" folder.
   - Hosting guide: https://chatwoot.com/deploy . Repo: https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot
   - You need a server and someone who can keep it updated and backed up. Chatwoot's own cloud is a paid product.
6. Cal.diy (instead of Calendly, a booking page). 50K+ stars. Licence: MIT.
   - Repo and install guide: https://github.com/calcom/cal.diy
   - PERSONAL USE ONLY: its own README says it is "strictly recommended for personal, non-production use" and that hosting it "requires advanced knowledge of server administration". For a business, the makers point to the paid Cal.com.
