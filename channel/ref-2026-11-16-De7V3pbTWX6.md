## Why it looks good and a bit Miki-like
- Light warm off-white base, black type, ONE accent (terracotta, the Claude colour), lots of air.
- Every screen is one card, centred, same width, same place: the eye never searches.
- Headline bar under the card shows 2-4 words at a time in heavy sans capitals, one accent word in colour.
- Motion is small and tied to content: lines typing into a draft, a gauge needle to 85.6, tiles flipping, a chart rising.
- Real material as proof: the real GitHub repo page, the real logos.

## What to copy (structure and layout)
1. One fixed stage: card in the same place every screen, headline bar in the same place.
2. A withheld "craziest one" beat at about two thirds (our build-up weak spot).
3. Each item gets its own small live demo (typing, a needle, a chart), not a static label.
4. The repo page screenshot as early proof (about 7-9 s).
5. A numbered setup card as the saveable screen.

## What NOT to copy (against `channel/miki-style-checklist-2026-11-16.md` or our rules)
- Thick black outlines and hard offset shadows on every box; the progress strip of icons at the top; filled chips and labels ("PAID PLAN", "FREE · MIT", "ILLUSTRATIVE"); the outlined caption box (Miki uses free-standing type); "comment SKILL" (we use link in bio); the hyperbole "killed Instagram" as a factual claim.
