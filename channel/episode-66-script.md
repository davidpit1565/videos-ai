## Claims verified live (9.10.2026, first-party)

aboutamazon.com/news/retail/how-to-check-amazon-price-history:
- "Customers can now see 30, 90, and 365 days of price history."
- "Click the price history link directly on any product detail page (right next to the price)."
- "Price history is currently available to all customers in the U.S., UK, Canada, and India." The 365-day view is still rolling out in the U.S., UK, India.
- "On May 13, 2026, Rufus was renamed Alexa for Shopping."
aboutamazon.com/news/retail/how-to-use-amazon-shopping-ai-assistant:
- "Alexa for Shopping shows 30-, 90-, and 365-day price history, so you can feel confident you're getting a great deal."
- Auto-buy: example prompt "Buy these headphones when they're 30% off"; it will "complete your purchase using your default payment method".
- "All U.S. Amazon customers have access to Alexa for Shopping."

## UNCONFIRMED — must NOT be said

- Prime requirement, 24-hour cancellation window, six-month duration of auto-buy (one search summary, no first-party page); spending limits;
  whether the price history is accurate; the exact click path for auto-buy; whether a confirmation step exists for price-target purchases.
- The video must not claim any "real deal" test result: it only shows where Amazon's own price history is.

## Spoken script (plain language)

1. Is that Amazon sale price real?
2. Amazon's own help page says it can show 30, 90 or 365 days of price history.
3. Open any product, and click the price history link, right next to the price.
4. It's Amazon's AI shopping tool, now called Alexa for Shopping.
5. It works in the US, UK, Canada and India.
6. It can even buy for you when the price hits your goal.
7. The page doesn't mention a spending limit.
8. Show this to someone who loves a sale.
9. The setup's in the link in bio. (locked)
10. Tomorrow, I'll show you how YouTube guesses your age. Follow, so you get the full story.
