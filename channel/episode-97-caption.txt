Same request to four Claude models: "Draw a pelican riding a bicycle."
Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5 and Fable 5.2. Every drawing in this video is the real code each model wrote, shown as it came out. One try each, no picking the best.

The smallest one forgets the seat. The newest one adds the sun, a shadow and a real bike.

The pelican test is Simon Willison's: he made it up in 2024 as a fun way to compare AI models. It does not measure everything an AI can do.
