## The fixed frame (the same on all 61 s)
- Background: off-white paper with a faint dot grid. One accent colour only: Claude orange. Graphify's green appears only on its logo and on the small "GRAPH" tags. Everything else is black ink on white: 2-3 px black borders and a hard black drop shadow on every card.
- Layout, top to bottom: (1) a "TOKENS LEFT" meter pinned to the top of the frame, (2) one changing "stage" card under it, (3) one caption pill under the stage (white, black border, 3-5 words at a time, the current word in orange, the words still to come in grey). The whole lower 45% of the frame is empty paper.
- The meter: a black "TOKENS LEFT" tab, a small pixel character in a square (a drawing of the Claude Code mascot), 10 orange blocks, a big percentage, a "GRAPH" / "NO GRAPH" tag on the right, and a tiny tag "illustrative". It is the spine of the video: it never leaves, and the viewer watches it drain and refill.
- Tiny source tags sit on most stages: "illustrative", "recreated", "claude.com/pricing · Nov 2026", "Source: GitHub, 6 Nov 2026", "the claim · varies by codebase", "best case: 71.5x per query, 52-file corpus · small repos ~1-5x", "README quick start".
- The pixel character is a guide: it moves to wherever the action is (sits on a card corner, scans a file list, walks the graph).

## What makes it feel alive
1. One persistent counter (the meter) that the viewer keeps checking. Every beat moves it, so even a static card has a number changing above it.
2. A new stage roughly every 1.5-3 s (about 22 stages in 61 s), and inside each stage things arrive one at a time (chips, rows, bars, ticks), never all at once.
3. Counters that count: the percentage, the star count rolling up, "RUNS 1", "0 ASKED", "READ x3".
4. Before/after pairs of the SAME picture (the week chart drained, then full; the file tray read, then "not re-read"), so the change is understood without words.
5. A small character that travels to where the viewer should look.
6. Tilted stickers and stamps that land on a beat ("THE FIX", "~30 SECONDS", "NOT RE-READ", "DONE", "COMMENT REPO").
7. Caption pill: 3-5 words, the spoken word in orange, the coming words grey.
8. Restraint: one accent colour, one font family for headings, a mono font only for "code" things.
9. Small source tags ("illustrative", "recreated", dated sources) make drawn pictures look checked. Note: the tags do not make the claims true. The reel still says "$20 does what $100 used to" and "70 times fewer tokens" as its message.

## Weak points (not to copy)
- The lower 45% of the frame is empty for the whole video (our rule: the main visual fills x 65-1015, y 269-1248).
- Code on screen (file names with .py, function lines, terminal commands): against our "no code on screen, plain words" rule.
- Caption pill = text repeated under the picture; our Miki style uses one headline instead.
- Its numbers are a best case shown as the headline (see `channel/ref-graphify-verify-2026-11-15.md`).
