## Prompt 1: "I want to start walking every day. What is the one thing I should do first?"
Decide when you'll walk, and tie it to something you already do every day.

For example: "After I finish my morning coffee, I put on my shoes and walk for 10 minutes." Pick the trigger and the time now, and keep the walk small enough that you can't talk yourself out of it. Ten minutes is plenty at the start.

Consistency matters more than distance in the first few weeks. A walk that always happens at the same moment of the day turns into a habit much faster than "I'll walk when I have time". You can lengthen it later, once showing up is automatic.

Tomorrow, do that first walk at the time you picked.
