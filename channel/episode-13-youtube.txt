Your ChatGPT has a tab you've probably never clicked

Right above the message box there's a toggle — Chat or Work — and most people have never
touched it. Switch it, describe what you actually need done, and ChatGPT breaks it into
steps and works through them — it can even browse a real website, log into it for you, and
check back instead of guessing.

The sign-in browsing part currently needs a Plus or Pro plan. The Work mode itself is open
to everyone.
