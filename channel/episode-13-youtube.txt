The tab next to ChatGPT's chat box you've never clicked

You've used ChatGPT a hundred times. You've never seen this: right above the message box
there's a toggle — Chat or Work. Switch it, describe what you actually need done, and
ChatGPT breaks it into steps and works through them — it can even browse a real website,
log into it for you, and check back instead of guessing.

The sign-in browsing part currently needs a Plus or Pro plan. The Work mode itself is open
to everyone.
