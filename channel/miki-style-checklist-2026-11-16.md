# Miki style checklist (written 16.11.2026, from the real frames)

Why: David, 16.11.2026: "we lost the Miki style." The reels that did best were 86 and 89 (89: 1,209 views, 18 saves; build recipe `video/ep89c/`). Episodes 98, 99, 100 and 102 v2 drifted to busier drawn animation. This file says what the style concretely is, from frames I looked at, not from memory.

Evidence (contact sheets in `channel/assets/miki-style-2026-11-16/`):
- `ep89-every-1.5s.jpg`: the published reel 89 (`studio/public/reels/reel-89.mp4` from origin/main), one frame every 1.5 s.
- `ep102v2-every-2.6s.jpg`: episode 102 v2, one frame every 2.6 s.
- `ep98-99-100-six-frames-each.jpg`: six frames each of 98 (v9 picture), 99 (v9) and 100 (v9).

## What episode 89 does (seen in `ep89-every-1.5s.jpg`)
1. ONE object on screen at a time. Either one dark card, or one block of words. Never a card plus a counter plus a stamp plus a caption.
2. The object is the real thing: the ChatGPT input box with the real prompt being typed, the real logo. No drawn characters, no icons that stand for something else.
3. Type has two levels only: a small lead-in line in regular weight ("When the response comes back,") and the key words in heavy black capitals ("IT TAKES A WHILE"). Words appear one at a time as they are spoken; the next word waits in pale grey.
4. One accent colour (terracotta), used once per screen at most, on the single word that matters ("SCARY.", "GO."), with one hand-drawn ring around it.
5. Whole screens are text only ("EVEN MORE SCARY.", "TEST IT OUT. GO."). Text screens and card screens alternate; they are not mixed.
6. A lot of calm space. The card is wide (about 920 of 1080 px), sits in the upper middle, soft shadow, no outline, no offset "sticker" shadow.
7. Motion is small and slow: typing, a soft blur between screens, a gentle drift. Nothing bounces, nothing slams, nothing wiggles while you read.
8. The eye has one place to go: top-left of the headline, then the card under it. The layout does not change from screen to screen.
9. Only one small label ("PROMPT 1"). No source lines, no footnotes, no numbers except what is spoken.

## What we drifted to (seen in `ep98-99-100-six-frames-each.jpg` and `ep102v2-every-2.6s.jpg`)
- Several things at once: 102 v2 shows a running total, a counter, six logo chips with ticks, a struck paid row, a twin card, a screenshot, a stamp and a source line on ONE frame.
- Drawn worlds and characters (98 the track and cars, 99 the maze and logo characters, 100 the weightlifter), thick ink outlines and hard offset shadows on every box.
- Rotated stamps ("BEST CASE", "PERSONAL USE ONLY", "NEW: 7 MONTHS OLD"), status tags, hazard-style labels.
- Many small texts (source lines at 24 px, "ILLUSTRATION" tags, star counts on every row).
- Bouncy motion: back-out pops, slams, idle wiggles on everything.
- A different layout per scene, so the eye has to search each time.

## DO
- One idea per screen. Before adding an element ask: would 89 have it? If not, leave it out.
- Alternate text screens and card screens. Do not put a headline block over a busy card.
- Lead-in line (regular) + heavy black capitals; words build as spoken; one accent word with one ring, at most once per screen.
- Real material as the content: real logos, real screenshots, real typed text. Show it large, in one soft dark or white card with rounded corners and a soft shadow.
- Keep one layout for repeated items: same place, same motion, every time.
- Slow, eased, small motion. Soft blur between screens.
- Numbers on screen only when they are the point of that screen.

## DO NOT
- No counters, running totals, progress strips, chips with ticks.
- No stamps, no rotated labels, no hard offset shadows, no thick ink outlines.
- No drawn characters, mascots, tracks, mazes, weights. (A one-off comedy beat needs David's yes first.)
- No source lines or footnotes on screen: they go in the caption and on the site page.
- No bounce, slam or wiggle. No element that moves only to "keep the frame alive".
- No two cards on one screen. No screenshot under a card under a headline.
- No word inside a filled box (standing rule; 89's "PROMPT 1" chip is the one thing of 89 we do not copy).

## Open point
The "something moves at all times" rule (1.11.2026) and this calm style pull against each other. In 89 the movement is the typing and the word-by-word build, not decoration: keep motion tied to the content.
