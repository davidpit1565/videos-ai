Your teacher says an AI wrote your essay. You wrote it. Here are 3 things that help you prove it.

1. Show your version history. In Google Docs: File, Version history, See version history.
2. Show your notes and drafts.
3. Ask the tool how often it is wrong.

Why bother? A Stanford study tested 7 other AI detectors on 91 essays written by people still learning English. About 6 in 10 were flagged as AI. Turnitin was not part of that test.

Turnitin says its detector has a false positive rate under 1% (Turnitin blog, March 2023). Vanderbilt University did the math on its own numbers: about 75,000 papers submitted in 2022 means around 750 could have been wrongly labeled. That is Vanderbilt's own estimate, not a count of real cases. Vanderbilt turned the detector off in August 2023.
