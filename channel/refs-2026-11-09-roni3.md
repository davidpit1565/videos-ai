## What this means for us: the level, faceless and our way
Honest scores (judgements, scale in channel/score-calibration.md; not measurements): reel 3 is the best match for our constraints (HOOK about 90, fully faceless, 18 s). Reel 1 and 2 hooks about 85-90 but their middle needs a face. Our current videos (light + lime base) are clean, but they read as "UI cards on a page"; none of the three references does that.

### What we cannot do (honest)
1. A host with a face and mic (reels 1 and 2 use him for 60% of the time). We keep a calm cloned voice and replace the face with object animation.
2. Real 3D rendered objects (glasses, column, key, magnifier, rocket): they come from a 3D tool/AI video model (Seedance, Blender, Spline). In HTML we can fake the look with PNG cut-outs with real shadows, but not true 3D rotation. ASSUMPTION: we may use a generated PNG with transparent background (the Adobe/Canva remove-background tools exist in this session, not tested here).
3. Real vintage photos of real people (Roosevelt, Ferrari, Musk): rights and "no real photo backgrounds" rule; Vox look needs archive cut-outs. We use public-domain archive imagery only when it IS the proof; otherwise drawn or object cut-outs. No people's faces from the web without checking rights.
4. The comment-keyword CTA works through DMs with a manual tool (ManyChat-like). Do we have it? Not verified in this repo. Our current CTA is "link in bio". Our rule on the final line (tomorrow) stays.
5. Account size and timing: the 12,746 likes are partly the account. A copy is not a guarantee: log as an experiment (experiments.md).

### Techniques we can build in HTML + GSAP + ffmpeg (concrete)
Camera: wrap each scene in a `.cam` div, animate `scale 1.00 -> 1.06` and `x/y` drift over the whole scene with `power2.inOut` or `sine.inOut` (never linear), `duration = scene length`; on a hard beat add a push `1.00 -> 1.12` in 0.35 s `expo.out`. Reel-3 style slam: scale 1.35 -> 1.00 in 0.25 s `expo.out` plus 6 px blur -> 0 (CSS `filter: blur()` animated).
Depth/parallax: 3 layers (background, object, foreground blurred object), each with a different drift speed (0.4x, 1x, 1.6x); the foreground object passing in front of the lens (reel 2 lamp, reel 3 glasses) is a PNG with `blur(8px)` crossing the frame in 0.6 s `power3.inOut` at the scene's start.
Focus pull on entry: `blur(14px) -> 0` over 0.5 s `power2.out` on the whole scene (reel 1, 0-1 s).
Text reveals (all available with GSAP + `motion-kit.js`):
 a) mixed-weight line: one `<div>` with `<span class=light>`, `<span class=heavy>`, words staggered 0.08 s, `y 30 -> 0`, `opacity 0 -> 1`, `rotation 3 -> 0`.
 b) hero word slam: `scale 1.5 -> 1`, `expo.out` 0.3 s, with a 4 px red `box-shadow` glow line (a 6 px high div) wiping left to right over 0.4 s, then fading.
 c) typing in torn strips: `gsap.to(el, {text: ..})` is a plugin; free alternative is our own char-by-char with random 35-70 ms spacing (like reel 1 "LOO / AT"), plus a blinking caret.
 d) scramble/glitch to final word: swap letters from a pool for 0.4 s then resolve (home-made 20-line function; no plugin needed), plus a 2 px chromatic offset (two copies in red/cyan with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`) for 0.15 s.
 e) mask reveal behind an object: `clip-path: inset(0 100% 0 0) -> inset(0)` over 0.45 s while the object moves.
 f) word cropped by the frame edge on purpose (reel 3): allowed only for decorative words; safe_check must skip it with `data-decor`; the key word stays inside x 65-1015.
 g) paper strip highlight: a rotated div (-2 deg) with a rough `clip-path: polygon(...)` edge and a jittered bottom edge; text inside; enters with `x -120% -> 0`, `power3.out`.
Texture: a looping film-grain PNG at 6-8% opacity with `mix-blend-mode: overlay` and a 4-frame jitter; paper/newsprint images need to be real, free-licence (Wikimedia Commons public domain scans, Unsplash). For the white "tech-drawing" look: SVG crosshairs + tiny mono text + faint grid lines, drawn by us (no licence issue).
Objects and shadows: PNG cut-out + `filter: drop-shadow(0 30px 25px rgba(0,0,0,.25))`, slow `rotateZ` 0 -> 4 deg, `rotateY` fake via `scaleX`; keep one hero object per scene.
Prompt-bar proof: our drawn UI with typed text and the send button, already in the repo style; the real product accent in the UI card (ChatGPT lime, Claude orange) as the CLAUDE.md rule says. Then make the background change behind it (reel 2, 4.5 s): a real before/after swap, tied to the typed word.
Rhythm: target 4-5 cuts per 10 s, a new thing every 1.5-2 s, and never a still frame over 2.5 s (existing rule). Use object-driven transitions (an object flies through the lens and becomes the next scene) instead of black wipes (Roni's own complaint on ep 83).
Layout: reels 1-2 use a split screen with the one-word caption on the seam; for our faceless version put the object animation across the full usable box (x 65-1015, y 269-1248), enlarged, as the CLAUDE.md "no wasted space" rule says. Caption chunks 1-2 words, centred, never over what the screen shows.
Fonts, free and open licence (Google Fonts, OFL), matched to what the references use: INVISIBLE / big word: Anton, Bebas Neue, or Big Shoulders Display (condensed black); high-contrast serif: Playfair Display (also italic), DM Serif Display, Fraunces; slab for strips: Alfa Slab One, Rokkitt, Roboto Slab Black; neo-grotesque: Inter, Archivo (variable width, nice for mixed weights), Instrument Sans; technical micro text: JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono; stencil: Stardos Stencil or Big Shoulders Stencil; handwriting: Caveat, Reenie Beanie. Use at most 3 families per episode: one condensed, one serif italic, one sans.
Colour: the references use ONE accent (yellow, or blood red) on a mostly neutral base, plus a single real product colour inside the UI card. Our "light + lime" base survives (reel 3 is also light), but the accent per episode should be strong and used on the one key word only, and our decoration must look like objects/paper rather than flat cards.

### Sound design plan (what to build; none of it is measured on us yet)
- A sfx library of real, licensed sounds (Pixabay sfx are free like our music library): whoosh (short, 0.3 s), paper slide/tear, glass shatter, soft sub-hit (impact), keyboard tick, UI blip, riser (1-2 s) before a reveal. ASSUMPTION: Pixabay has them; not checked here and the repo has no sfx folder (to check before building).
- Cue map per beat: hook slam word = sub-hit + short whoosh at the same frame; each object entering = whoosh 0.15 s before it lands; each typed prompt = tick per 2-3 letters at low gain; background swap = whoosh up + soft thud; stat/card = UI blip; every scene change = whoosh or paper slide matched to the cut; last CTA = a riser into a final hit.
- Levels: voice stays at -14 LUFS master as the reels do; sfx sit about 6-10 dB under the voice peaks, never masking words; bed 18-20 dB under (existing rule) but with a low-band (<120 Hz) lift under hits: reel 3 has about 8 LU more low-band energy than the others, which is what makes it feel "heavy" on a phone speaker.
- A cloned calm voice cannot get the host's energy: compensate with tighter cuts and sfx on every beat, which the reference reels also do through visuals.

### Beat by beat: what we copy
1. 0.0 s: first frame is a MOVING object with the subject in 2-4 big words that state the topic plus the problem ("Your content is INVISIBLE" level of clarity). Our own topic, product logo on frame 1 (existing rule).
2. 0.5-2.0 s: one slam word with an accent glow line + sub-hit sfx; a foreground object crosses the lens.
3. 2-5 s: the one visible proof or the "what happens" prompt typed live, and the visible result changes (background swap, number changes).
4. 5-10 s: first payoff arrives (the thing to copy or save appears early, per our rule); the object-driven transition into the explanation.
5. 10-end: one new object/visual every 1.5-2 s; keep key words in the mixed-weight headline; no talking head.
6. End: keyword CTA only if we can serve the DM; otherwise "setup in link in bio" + tomorrow line (CLAUDE.md order: link line second to last, tomorrow line last). The keyword mechanic (comment X) is a candidate EXPERIMENT, not a given: log it in experiments.md.
7. Loop: bookend with the hook object (reel 2's Tesla animation returns at the end).

### Honest verdict
Reaching reel 3's look is possible only in part: the typography moves, camera, mask reveals, glitch, mixed weights, grain, shadows and sound design are all within HTML+GSAP+ffmpeg. The real 3D objects and the vintage-photo cut-outs are the gap; they require generated or sourced assets (check rights and the "no real photo backgrounds" rule: photos only when they are the proof). A first test should be a single 18 s episode built this way, with a blind first-frame test, logged in experiments.md; if the honest panel score is under 85, do not ship (CLAUDE.md).
