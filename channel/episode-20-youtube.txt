ChatGPT was going to buy things for you — then they quietly killed it

One year ago the pitch was "buy it in ChatGPT, no more leaving the chat." Six months
after that, OpenAI retired the feature. The real number behind the shutdown: fewer
than fifteen of Shopify's millions of merchants ever turned it on, and buying inside
ChatGPT converted at roughly a third the rate of just sending the shopper to the
store directly.

What's left now is product discovery, not purchase: ChatGPT finds what you want and
hands you a link to go buy it yourself — the same thing a search engine has done
since 2010. The upgrade, in the end, was a link.
