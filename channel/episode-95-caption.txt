Ask ChatGPT to draw you. Here's how:

1. Open ChatGPT.
2. Type: "Create a caricature of me and my job based on everything you know about me."
3. Watch it draw you.

Tip: tell it a bit about yourself first, and skip uploading your real photo. The more private details you give an AI, the more it keeps.

The drawing in this video is a drawn example, not a real ChatGPT result.
