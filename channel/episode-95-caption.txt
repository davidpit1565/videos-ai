Your ChatGPT caricature comes with a privacy warning.

The trend: ask ChatGPT "Create a caricature of me and my job based on everything you know about me." (Prompt wording as published by Forbes, 9 Feb 2026.)

What Forbes reports (Kate O'Flaherty, 9 Feb 2026):
- The trend took over social media in February.
- A security CEO says users keep adding personal details until the result looks right.
- Experts advise skipping real photos and keeping the prompt generic.
- Memory (referencing saved memories and chat history) is off by default for users in the UK and EU, Forbes wrote in February. Check your own settings.
- Safer: describe yourself in words, or use Temporary Chat.

The drawing in this video is a drawn example, not a real ChatGPT result. Everything else comes from the Forbes article.
