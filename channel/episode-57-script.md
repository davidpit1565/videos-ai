### Proactive frozen-picture fix (applied before first render, per episode 56's lesson)

Scenes 2 and 6 both use `ep57-scene-b-phoneglow.jpg`, whose background is a large,
near-uniform cream/beige gradient around a small high-contrast phone silhouette — the
same photo-content property episode 56's AirPods macro shot had (uniform/bokeh regions
produce near-zero frame-to-frame diff at `qa.py`'s downscaled measurement resolution
regardless of applied motion size). Applied a plain LINEAR (no eased tail), larger-
magnitude pan/zoom on these two scenes specifically, before the first render rather than
after a failed check — scenes with real texture throughout (A/C/D/E) keep the normal
eased `MK.kenBurns()`.

### Voice round 1

Raised-energy settings per David's standing instruction this round (Nas-Daily-style
enthusiasm): `build_voice.py --cues video/reel-57.html --out audio/voice/ep57.wav
--exaggeration 0.70 --cfg 0.30 --seed 57`. Output: 45.3s, 8 lines, all canonical/locked
lines reused byte-for-byte per the manifest.

`voice_doctor.py --deep` found two real, energy-induced issues, matching the exact
failure mode documented in this round's brief:
1. **"camera" (line 1, 1.48s) and "called" (line 2, 7.64s) measured rushed/clipped**
   (0.067s and 0.090s per syllable against a 0.200s median) — flagged "likely
   swallowed, re-roll this line."
2. **Line 6's ending measured weak** (-60.6 dB vs. the file's own -36.3 dB median) —
   the line's final word ("job.") read soft.

Fix applied: (a) line 6 reworded to end on a harder stop consonant instead of pulling
back energy alone — "...it does a solid job." → "...it gets the job done." (re-linted
clean, 0 risky words), (b) `--line-exaggeration "1:0.58,2:0.58,6:0.58"` pulls lines 1,
2 and 6 back toward baseline while every other line keeps the full 0.70 energy, per
the brief's documented fix (reword where a clean rewrite exists, pull back energy
where it doesn't).

### Rounds 2-20 — the real, honest account

This took far more iteration than any prior episode, and it's logged in full rather
than smoothed over. Rounds 2-10 (seeds 57-63, various `--line-exaggeration` combos)
each cleared whichever specific word had failed the round before, but a *different*
word somewhere else in the file would come up rushed/clipped instead — "called,"
"already," "uses," "camera," "front," "regular" each failed in rotation across
different lines and different seeds, never converging. Line 3's accent also drifted
(`not-american 0.84-0.92` vs. a file median of ~0.01) on two separate seeds.

**Root-cause finding, confirmed by direct test:** dropping the whole file's energy
back to the plain locked baseline (`--exaggeration 0.50`, no per-line overrides at
all) *still* produced the exact same two failures (line 1's opening "Google" held
unnaturally long, and line 3's "uses" swallowed) — proving these two specific
problems were never energy-related at all, just seed-sensitive for this script's
exact wording. That changed the fix strategy: energy level stayed at 0.70 base
(restored once the real culprits were found), and the two genuinely recurring
words got fixed directly —
- Line 1: "...point it at anything, and it talks back..." → "...point it at
  whatever's in front of you, and it talks back..." (removed "anything," entirely —
  this also incidentally dropped "camera" from line 1's wording later in the same
  round, since the sentence was restructured).
- Line 2: "It's called Search Live..." → "It's Search Live..." (dropped "called,"
  which failed in 3 of 4 attempts regardless of seed).
- Line 2 continued: "...and it's already built into the regular Google app" →
  "...built right into the main Google app" (dropped both "already" and "regular,"
  each of which failed independently in later rounds).
- Line 3: "...and it uses your camera and your voice..." → "...and it watches and
  listens through your camera and your voice..." (dropped "uses," the one word that
  reproduced the identical swallow at the identical timestamp across 3 separate
  seeds, including at the plain locked baseline).

**Final accepted take (round 20):** `--exaggeration 0.70 --cfg 0.30 --seed 61
--line-seeds "1:900,2:909,3:2024"` (no `--line-exaggeration` needed in the end — the
real fixes were the rewording, not the energy pullback). `voice_doctor.py --deep`:
clean line table, "nothing flagged," zero rushed/clipped words, zero held-too-long
extremes. `check_accent.py`: "nothing stands out from this file's own baseline" on
every line, including line 3.

### Frozen-picture BLOCKER, photo swap (not just a motion-magnitude fix)

First full `check.sh` run found two real BLOCKERs: scene 1
(`ep57-scene-a-pointing.jpg`, a rock/bokeh photo) frozen 4.43s, and scene 3
(`ep57-scene-c-street.jpg`, a shallow-DOF phone-vs-blurred-background photo) frozen
5.83s — the same uniform-texture failure mode episode 56 found on a different photo.
**Tried and empirically ruled out first:** nearly tripling both scenes' Ken Burns
magnitude (scale 1.0→1.32/1.34, translate -4.5%/-5.0%) and re-rendering — the BLOCKER
barely moved (4.43s→4.17s, 5.83s→4.17s), confirming again that motion magnitude alone
cannot fix a photo whose crop window traverses mostly-uniform content, regardless of
how large the motion is.

**Fix: swapped both photos for genuinely different ones** — sourced via live web
search (Unsplash), measured for real frame-to-frame diff at `qa.py`'s own downscaled
resolution via `export/frames.js` before committing, not just eyeballed:
- Scene 1 → a DUMBO/Manhattan Bridge street photo (deep focus throughout, person
  photographing the bridge), real Adobe face-detection run (bbox y=0.55, well clear
  of the headline zone).
- Scene 3 → a Bleecker St, NYC pedestrian-crossing photo (dense, sharp, detailed
  streetscape), real Adobe face-detection run (bbox y=0.66, clear).
Both uploaded to Adobe Creative Cloud and checked with `image_select_subject`
(`bodyParts:["Face"], returnBbox:true`) per the mandatory gate — neither photo's face
overlaps the fixed headline zone. Ken Burns magnitude reverted back to normal
(scale ~1.16-1.18) once the real photos were in place — the aggressive zoom was only
needed as a (failed) workaround for the old photos and wasn't needed once the content
itself had real texture. Re-ran the full gate: both BLOCKERs cleared to non-blocking
"nearly still" warns. Scene 1's photo is also reused (non-adjacent) in scene 5, so
that scene's visual quality improved as a side effect.

### Retime `--tail` parameter mismatch (not a content bug)

The first retime attempt refused to write output ("timings left on the old timeline")

against the old scene's `data-out`, not guessed — fixed by passing `--tail 1.1` to
match this build's own authored buffer.
