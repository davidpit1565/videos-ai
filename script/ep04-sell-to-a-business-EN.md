# Episode 04 — "The AI setup I would sell to a business"

**Format:** long-form YouTube (target 18–24 min) + a 50s reel
**Topic:** the `sell` multiplier — the word that appeared 5× more often in the top quartile of
the measured set than any other. Not a search topic of its own; a lens on all of them.
**Why fourth:** episodes 02 and 03 build the credibility this one spends. It is the first
episode whose job is a client enquiry rather than a view.

---

## BLOCKED, and this one is not negotiable

**This episode cannot be made until there is one real client engagement.**

Without one, every number in it is invented, and an invented saving is the single thing that
kills a channel like this in one video. The format is a case study; a case study with no case
is a sales pitch wearing a case study's clothes, and the audience for this topic has seen
hundreds of those.

**What unblocks it:** one business, one process, one before-and-after that was actually
measured. Nothing else. Not a bigger client, not a better story — one real one.

Until then this file is a plan, and it says so.

---

## The shape, once there is a client

| Section | What is on screen |
|---|---|
| The business | What it does, how many people, in one minute. Named or anonymised — their call |
| The process, before | The actual steps, timed. A stopwatch, not an estimate |
| Where it broke | The step that cost the most, and why it was that one |
| What I built | The build, at speed, with the prompt readable |
| The process, after | Same stopwatch, same task |
| **What it did not fix** | The steps still manual, and the one thing that got slightly worse |
| The arithmetic | Hours saved × loaded cost, minus what it costs to run. Formula on screen |
| The price, and why | Tied to the number above, said out loud |
| What I would not have built | The thing they asked for that I talked them out of |

## The rules this episode runs on

- **Every figure comes from the client's own data or a stopwatch on camera.** No industry
  averages, no "businesses typically".
- **The formula stays on screen while the number is said.** Hours saved × loaded hourly cost,
  minus ongoing cost. If the inputs are ranges, the answer is a range.
- **The section on what it did not fix is not optional**, and it is not last. It sits before
  the arithmetic, because a viewer who hears the savings first stops listening.
- **The price is said, not hinted.** A video about selling that will not name a number is the
  thing it is criticising.
- **If the ROI does not support the price, that is the episode.** Saying "this was not worth
  what I charged" once is worth more than ten videos that never risk it.

## The number that decides whether this ships

**Payback in months** — implementation cost ÷ monthly net benefit — from their figures.
If it comes out worse than roughly a year, the episode is about why the project was the wrong
one, and that version is *more* useful than the success version. Either way it is honest, and
either way there is a video.

## The reel cut (50s)

Hook is the arithmetic, not the outcome: *"I charged them €X. Here's the maths I showed them
first."* The reel carries one number and the formula behind it; the long cut carries the build.

## Not in this episode

- A price list. There is no price list — the file `roi-proposal` says why, and pricing this
  client is not pricing the next one.
- Any claim about what the business earned afterwards. We measure what we changed, not what
  their market did.
- Other clients. When there is one, this episode is about one.
