# Evidence base (20.10.2026) — grades: A replicated/meta-analytic or primary with method; B solid single study or primary without method; C vendor/blog/secondary; D untraceable

## Platform statements
- TikTok Newsroom, "How TikTok recommends videos for you" (B): signals = user interactions, video information, device/account settings (lower weight); weighted by value to the user; finishing a longer video counts more than a weak signal; early replays/likes start an early distribution round. No ranking list, no weights.
- TikTok creative best practices (B, written for ads): hook in the first 6 s, proposition in the first 3 s, a clear call to action; text pacing "5-10 words per second" is for ad text.
- Instagram via Mosseri, reported by blogs (C): watch time, likes and sends per reach are the three most important signals (Jan 2025); likes slightly more for followers, sends slightly more for non-followers; "sends correlate with reach more than anything" (May 2024, unverified quote).
- YouTube blog (B): from 24.8.2026 views counted from the first frame; engaged views unchanged; recommendations unchanged.

## Psychology
- Loewenstein 1994, Psych. Bulletin 116(1): curiosity as information gap (B). Golman and Loewenstein 2018, Decision 5(3): gaps are felt when specific questions are in mind (B).
- Kang et al. 2009 Psych. Science; Gruber, Gelman, Ranganath 2014 Neuron: curiosity, reward circuitry, memory (B; small fMRI).
- Itti and Baldi (NIPS 2005; Vision Research 2009): Bayesian surprise predicts gaze in natural video (B).
- Moray 1959; Wood and Cowan 1995; Conway et al. 2001: own-name capture in about a third of people (B). Symons and Johnson 1997: self-reference memory meta-analysis (A for memory).
- Soroka, Fournier, Nir 2019 PNAS: negativity bias in arousal, 17 countries, 1,156 people, up to ~40% no bias (B+).
- Lang LC4MP line; Wise et al. 2008 found no orienting response after cuts (contested, B-/C).
- Alter and Oppenheimer 2009: processing fluency (B, abstract level).
- Newton 1990 tapping study: curse of knowledge, 2.5% guessed vs 50% predicted (B, via summaries).
- No peer-reviewed study found measuring first-seconds drop-off in short video with eye tracking or EEG.

## Contested or myths
- Zeigarnik: Ghibellini and Meier 2025 meta-analysis, memory advantage not replicated; resumption urge (Ovsiankina) held up.
- 8-second goldfish: 2015 report, did not trace to its source.
- Loss aversion ~2x: Brown, Imai, Vieider, Camerer 2024 (607 estimates, mean ~1.96); Gal and Rucker 2018 dispute; money choices only.
- Facebook/Nielsen "47% of value in 3 s": commissioned, unclear metric. Facebook IQ 2016 average mobile feed attention 1.7 s: 10 years old, feed not Reels, secondary.
- Clickbait and trust: three studies/surveys lower perceived credibility (news headlines); Munger et al. 2020 found null effects. Not video.

## Hooks on AI content
- Few verifiable cases. Third-party only (C/D): "POV: You wake up in 1351 during the Black Plague" (AI-made, vendor-reported 19.5M and 21.8M views); "YOUR AI VIDEOS LOOK SO FAKE" (overlay hook, no numbers).
- Pattern reported for AI educators (C): show the tool, the prompt, the result.
- Vendor structure claims (D): numbered ~30%, curiosity gap ~25%, contrarian ~15% highest engagement (500 faceless Shorts, unverified); showcase hooks ~2x lowest type (34,635 clips, vendor).

## Corpus (n=43, ChatGPT tip videos on TikTok, public stats read 20.10.2026)
- Sample = scraper list of videos that already did well (median 760k plays). Not a sample of all videos.
- Medians: saves 3.3%, shares 0.85%, likes 5.9%, duration 80 s.
- Crude hook features (first-person, "you", superlative, "prompt/hack/tip") did not separate high from low at n=43.
- Top video, 12.2M plays, 2.6% shares: a first-person story of a conversation with ChatGPT.
- Savable how-to videos reached 4-7% saves.

## Data study (20.10.2026) — grade A numbers, B conclusions
- 98 AI channels, 5,341 YouTube Shorts (latest 60 each, <=61 s), views relative to each channel's own median. Within-channel effect of title features: tool named x1.2, number x1.13, how-to x1.2, "free" x1.3, caps word x1.26, "you/your" x0.9, negative framing x0.85; question, first person, hype words, emoji, length: no effect. 22 title features together: R-squared 0.015 within channel. Raw data: `channel/hook-corpus-youtube-shorts-ai-20-10-2026.csv`.
- Not measured: spoken hooks (YouTube blocked audio access), Reels, retention, saves/shares on YouTube.
