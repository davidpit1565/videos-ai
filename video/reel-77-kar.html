Your phone hides a map pin inside your photos!

The pin is a set of coordinates, saved inside the photo file.

Send one, and the person who gets it may see where it was taken.

OpenAI says ChatGPT does not read that hidden data.

It only looks at the picture itself. And a picture can still give a place away.

To keep it off, tap the send arrow, then Options, and switch Location off.

The setup's in the link in bio.
