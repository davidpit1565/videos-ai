Your Instagram views are losing to TikTok

Two platforms writing into one shared field is a silent bug: whichever sync runs second wins, with no error and no warning. Shows up in Instagram + TikTok trackers, Reels + Shorts merged into one views column, any multi-platform report built in n8n, Zapier, or a spreadsheet. The fix: give every platform its own field, never share one.
