Episode 11

The check that hears what you can't

Every episode's narration is AI-generated, and AI narration has its own defects — a word that loses its ending, an S that goes dull or too hot. voice_doctor.py measures those straight from the audio, grades every line against this narration's own median, and blocks the file if one line overlaps the next. --repair fixes the level and the S, then checks its own fix until a pass finds nothing left.
