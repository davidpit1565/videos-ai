# Round 5 (1.11.2026): story with voice, sound effects and music
`story2.py` builds the 23-39 s story from cue times (HyperFrames render); `mix.py` lays David's narration lines (one wav per line, built by `audio/build_voice.py`, not stored here) at the cues, adds synthesized sound effects (pop, click, error buzzer, success chime, whoosh) and a music bed from `audio/music-library/` (FORCE_TRACK confident--innovation--atlasaudio), then loudnorm (two-pass, about -15.8 LUFS measured, target -14). Themes: 6works (light, lime, ChatGPT logo wheels), 4chatgpt (dark, green), 9navy (softer blue), 10phone (phone frame, blue messages). `claude-symbol.svg` is the real Claude symbol from Wikimedia Commons ("Claude AI symbol.svg"). Not integrated with `export/produce.sh` and its gates: a shipped reel still has to go through them.
