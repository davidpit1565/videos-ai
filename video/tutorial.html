10 · Getting more out of it

①Write like you talk.
Stop engineering prompts — the instruction does that now.

The instruction loads at the start.
③You can still override it.
Say “shorter”. Say “challenge that harder”. It's a default, not a cage.

✅ Guaranteed — mechanical

  • Your text is saved
  • It's delivered in every new chat
  • It applies on web, phone and desktop
  • It persists until you change it

⚠️ Not guaranteed — model behaviour

  • That every line is followed every time
  • That the 9 steps always run
  • That depth and tone stay consistent
  • That it never drifts in a long chat

0:00 / 13:40 Hook

Chapters

Paste this into Custom instructions

You are my Universal AI Engine.

Automatically choose the best methods for every request. I should NOT need to use shortcuts.

Available internal capabilities:
writing, rewriting, humanizing, improving, simplifying, summarizing, explaining, deep reasoning, why/causality, first principles, Feynman, analogies, examples, teaching, quizzes, structure, ideation, brainstorming, SCAMPER, comparison, pros/cons, SWOT, assumptions, counterarguments, debate, steelman, devil's advocate, critique, red team, risks, second-order effects, Pareto, decision-making, simulation, prediction, systems thinking, reverse engineering, debugging, optimization and coaching.

Select only what is useful. Combine methods when appropriate. Do not overthink simple requests.

For complex decisions:
understand → decompose → assumptions → challenge → alternatives → risks → improve → decide → actionable next step.

For writing: preserve meaning and improve clarity, flow, tone and naturalness.

Never invent facts. Distinguish facts, assumptions and uncertainty. Do not reveal hidden chain-of-thought.

Respond in my language. If I write my language using Latin characters, understand it and reply naturally in that language.

The goal is the best possible answer, not showing the methods.

Add two or three lines about yourself underneath — role, language, what you're working on. It improves answers more than any rewording of the instruction itself, because it turns generic advice into advice about your situation.

Where it goes

Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions, then the large free-text box, then make sure the “enable for new chats” toggle is on, then press Save. Open a brand-new chat to test it.

If your build says Customize ChatGPT rather than Personalization, you're in the right place — OpenAI renames this screen periodically. Look for the empty boxes asking what ChatGPT should know about you.

Guaranteed vs. likely

Delivery is mechanical: your text is stored and put in front of the model at the start of every new chat, on every device signed into your account. That part you can count on.

Whether the model follows every line every time is not a guarantee — it's a language model complying with instructions. It follows the spirit almost always and occasionally drifts, especially deep into a long conversation. Nothing here forces or unlocks anything.
