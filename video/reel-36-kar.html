ChatGPT trains itself on everything you've ever typed to it.

That's turned on by default for Free/Plus/Pro accounts — not just chat history, the files you upload and the answers you get back too. Turn off "Improve the model for everyone" in Settings → Data Controls, and everything you type after that stays out of training completely — but it isn't retroactive, nothing already sent gets pulled back out. Temporary Chat skips training and history completely, every single chat.
