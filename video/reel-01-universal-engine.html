Actually Works — Episode 01

Rebuilt after the first cut ran flat. Same information, different physics.

What changed and why

  • Nothing is ever still. A drifting colour field behind everything, and every scene slowly pushes in. A static frame reads as a slideshow, and people swipe past slideshows.
  • Twice the beat rate. Something enters, changes or leaves roughly every 1.2 seconds. 62s → 55s with more in it.
  • Captions carry the emphasis. One word per line goes brass. On mute — which is most viewers — that word is the whole message.
  • A progress bar. Visible progress measurably reduces mid-video drop-off.
  • Answers now expire. Old blocks leave as new ones arrive, so the frame never turns into a wall of text.
  • Music. Synthesised per video, exactly the right length, and impossible to copyright-claim.

Still the weak link

The voice. It's a local neural TTS and it sounds like one. Two fixes, in order of how much they'd help: record it yourself — your voice, no camera, free, and instantly better than any synthetic option — or ElevenLabs at a few dollars a month. Everything else in the pipeline stays identical; only the WAV changes.
