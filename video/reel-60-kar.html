Anything you type to Instagram's AI chat can come back as an ad.

Since late 2025, Meta — the company behind Instagram — uses your chats with its AI to pick your ads and posts.

Ask about a trip, and trip ads can follow. Meta says sensitive topics, like religion or politics, stay out of ads.

Here's the catch. No switch can stop it. Reports say Europe and the UK are left out. Most places aren't.

If you use the Meta AI app, you can wipe your chats: Menu, Settings, Data and privacy, then Delete all chats.

Meta doesn't say if that changes your ads. So pause, then type. Treat it like a search box.
