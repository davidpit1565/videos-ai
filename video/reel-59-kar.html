Your LinkedIn profile is already teaching an AI — and no one asked you.

It has an option that lets it train its AI on your profile and your posts. And it's on by default.

Your job titles, your skills, your summary, your comments — all of it can be used. Your login and your bank info stay out of it.

Switching it off takes under a minute. Open LinkedIn, tap your photo, then Settings, then Data privacy.

Tap Data for Generative AI Improvement, and flip the switch off.

One honest catch. Switching it off only works from now on. What it already learned, it keeps.
