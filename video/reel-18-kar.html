Episode 18

ChatGPT can work through a website on its own now.

ChatGPT Work — the mode next to Chat — opens real websites, reads them, and comes back with a finished result: a spreadsheet, a comparison, a report. It won't log into an account for you, and it isn't instant — a real task can run for hours, and it's part of your existing plan's usage, not a separate unlimited tool. Give it one task at a time, watch it run, then decide if you trust the rest.
