Reel 01 · v3 · 52s · his voice

Built to stop the scroll

v2 was elegant. Elegant doesn't stop a thumb. This version is designed for a phone held at arm's length, in daylight, with the sound off.

What changed

  • It opens on words, not a screenshot. Frame one is a full-bleed sentence at 13cqw — readable in the 0.4s someone gives you before deciding.
  • Emphasis is a filled block, not coloured text. A brass box around the key word survives a small, bright screen; coloured text doesn't.
  • Captions are ~30% bigger and sit higher, clear of the Instagram UI.
  • Section flashes. A 0.35s full-frame colour card between sections — a hard pattern interrupt that resets attention five times in 52 seconds.
  • Brighter, higher contrast. The palette was raised and a second accent added, so the frame has heat instead of only mood.
  • A countdown during setup. Visible urgency while the least dramatic part of the video plays.
  • Cut to his voice, not the other way round. Every beat is aligned to the actual recording.
