Episode 02 · Reel

What an AI agent actually is

The word is everywhere and the line is nowhere. This one draws it in fifty seconds: a chatbot answers, an agent runs a loop — plan, act with a real tool, check — and the test for telling them apart is whether it can act without you.

Why this episode, here

Measured demand puts “ai agents explained” at a median of 124,549 views, an order of magnitude above the custom-instructions topic episode 01 sits on. It is also the gateway: every later episode assumes the viewer knows what a loop is.

The part nobody else shows

How agents fail. Not “sometimes it hallucinates” — the three real ones: the confident wrong recipient, the half-finished task reported as done, the paid action run twice. That is why the first agent anyone builds should touch something reversible.
