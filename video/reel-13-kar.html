Episode 13

Your Instagram views might be wrong right now

Picture one box. Instagram writes a number into it. A moment later, TikTok writes its own number into that exact same box — and TikTok's number erases Instagram's, completely. No warning, no error, the box just shows a number that looks normal. Happens with Instagram and TikTok tracked together, Reels and Shorts counted together, any two things sharing one spot instead of two. The fix: give each one its own box, never let them share.
