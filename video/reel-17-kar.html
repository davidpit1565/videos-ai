A 5-second test for any explanation you write.

One undefined word — "column" — is enough to lose someone who already knows the topic. The fix isn't writing more carefully, it's a real test: read the line out loud, ask "what did I just say, in your own words?" Never yes or no. If the answer doesn't come back right, the line isn't done. The whole test lives in a free skill, explain-steps, downloadable below.
