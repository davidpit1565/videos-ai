Episode 20

ChatGPT was going to buy things for you.

OpenAI launched Instant Checkout in September 2025 — buy something without ever leaving the chat. Six months later they retired it: fewer than fifteen of Shopify's millions of merchants ever turned it on, and buying inside ChatGPT converted at about a third the rate of just sending the shopper to the store. What's left is product discovery — it finds what you want, then hands you a link, same as a search engine always did.
