# Visual Generation Prompts

For AI image/motion tools (Midjourney, Firefly, Runway, Sora, Kling, Veo) and for motion
designers working in After Effects. Every prompt below is written to land in the same
visual system: near-black background, indigo/teal accents, flat vector, no photorealism,
no text baked into the image (add all text in the edit so it stays legible and localisable).

**Universal style suffix — append to every image prompt:**
> flat vector illustration, minimal, near-black background #0B0D10, indigo #6366F1 and teal #2DD4BF accents, soft ambient glow, generous negative space, no text, no watermark, no logos, modern premium tech-editorial aesthetic, 16:9

**Universal negative prompt:**
> photorealistic, stock-photo people, cluttered, neon cyberpunk, glowing brain, humanoid robot, circuit-board patterns, binary code, blue-and-orange gradient clichés, text, letters, watermark, busy background

> **Avoid the AI-video clichés:** no glowing blue brains, no android faces, no Matrix rain, no handshake-between-human-and-robot. This video's look is calm editorial diagram, not sci-fi.

## S2 · The assistant and the sticky note

**Still (Midjourney / Firefly):**
> A simple flat-vector desk scene: one seated figure at a minimal desk, rendered as clean geometric shapes with no facial detail. A single yellow sticky note is stuck to the desk surface, slightly raised with a soft shadow. Calm and warm, not corporate. [style suffix]

**Motion (Runway / Kling, image-to-video):**
> Slow gentle loop. The figure shifts slightly, breathing. The yellow sticky note settles onto the desk with a soft drop and a small bounce, then stays perfectly still. Camera locked off, no push. 4 seconds, seamless loop.

## S2 · Orbiting capabilities

**Motion design brief (After Effects, not AI-generated — needs precise text):**
> A rounded rectangle in centre frame. ~40 small pill-shaped chips orbit it in three concentric elliptical rings at different speeds (outer slowest). Chips are semi-transparent white with 1px borders. On cue, three named chips brighten to solid indigo, break orbit and glide into the centre box, which pulses once on absorb. The remaining chips continue orbiting, slightly dimmed.

## S4 · Three-layer stack

**Motion design brief:**
> Three horizontal slabs in isometric-lite perspective (12° tilt), stacked with visible gaps. Built bottom-up, 500ms apart. Bottom slab: matte grey with a small closed-padlock icon. Middle slab: indigo, internally lit, with a slow shimmer and a pencil icon. Top slab: clean white. Camera then pushes into the middle slab, which opens like a drawer to reveal scrolling text inside.

**AI-video alternative:**
> Three translucent glass panels floating in dark empty space, stacked horizontally with soft depth of field. The middle panel glows warm indigo from within. Slow camera dolly toward the middle panel. Cinematic, minimal, no text. 6 seconds.

## S4 · The wrap animation *(the key visual — do not delegate to AI video)*

**Motion design brief:**
> A small white card sits centred, containing a single short line of text. A large indigo bracket draws itself around the card over 600ms, opening outward. As it opens, dense small-type instruction text flows in to fill the space between bracket and card, wrapping the card completely. The card itself never changes — emphasise this by keeping it perfectly static while everything around it moves.

## S4 · Nine-step pipeline

**Motion design brief:**
> Nine circular nodes connected by thin lines, flowing left to right, wrapping to two rows. Each node illuminates in sequence with a 180ms stagger; the connecting line fills with light between them like a fuse. After the last node lights, the whole chain dims to 15% opacity and slides behind a clean white answer card that rises from the bottom.

## S3 · Two-lane comparison

**Motion design brief:**
> Vertical split, thin 1px divider. Left lane tinted neutral grey and labelled; right lane tinted indigo. Both lanes animate a simplified chat exchange simultaneously. In the left lane, a thought-bubble with a scrolling list appears above the user before they type — visual shorthand for "you had to decide first." In the right lane, three capability pills fly in from off-screen left and merge into the message bubble with a soft absorb pulse. Both lanes land on visually identical answer cards.

## S10 · Chatbot vs Agent

**Motion design brief — the clearest graphic in the video:**
> Left half: a single speech bubble. A question mark enters from the left, an exclamation exits to the right. One beat, static, repeating.
> Right half: a small geometric figure picks up a task card, walks along a dotted path visiting three labelled tool icons in sequence (magnifying glass, browser window, calendar), each icon pulsing as it's used, then returns to the start and sets down a completed card with a checkmark. Loop 6 seconds.

**AI-video alternative:**
> A minimal geometric character walking along a glowing dotted path in dark empty space, stopping at three floating translucent icons in sequence, each lighting up as it passes. Flat vector aesthetic, side-on view, locked camera. 6 seconds, seamless loop.

## S9 · Second-order effects branch

**Motion design brief:**
> A single node labelled with the decision. One line grows downward to a second node, then branches into two, then those branch again — a small tree growing over 2 seconds with organic ease. As it grows, the earliest node stays bright and the newest nodes glow amber. A time-axis line runs along the bottom, marked from *launch* to *3 years*.

---

## S12 · The guarantee split

**Motion design brief:**
> A balance beam, centred, initially level. Two panels hang from it. The left panel fills with solid green-checked items that stack with weight; the right panel fills with amber items that appear translucent and slightly drift. The beam tips gently left and settles — deliberately *not* a dramatic tip. The visual should read as "one of these is solid, one is likely", not "one is good, one is bad".

## Thumbnail prompts (test at least three)

1. > Split composition: left side a plain grey chat bubble looking flat and empty, right side the same bubble rendered rich with layered indigo and teal data-shapes spilling out of it. Bold central negative space for text. High contrast, readable at 320px wide. [style suffix]
2. > A single yellow sticky note pinned to a dark surface, glowing faintly from within, casting a long soft shadow — implying enormous power in a small note. Extreme minimalism, dramatic side lighting. [style suffix]
3. > A cheat-sheet card covered in slash-commands, torn cleanly in half, the halves floating apart against near-black. Behind the tear, a clean warm indigo glow. [style suffix]

Thumbnail text options (keep to 3–5 words, ≥ 120px tall):
`ONE PASTE.` · `NO SHORTCUTS.` · `PASTE THIS ONCE.`

## Voice / narration generation

**English VO (ElevenLabs or similar):**
- Voice profile: warm mid-range, 30s–40s, neutral or light British/American. Not breathy, not "announcer".
- Settings guidance: stability ~50, similarity ~75, style exaggeration low. High stability makes tutorials sound dead; too low makes them wander.
- Render **per scene**, not as one file — you will re-cut individual lines.
